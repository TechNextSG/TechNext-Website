# Team cards (Team Directory /employee-hub + home #advisers) — surface brief

Scope: the team-member card and its quick view, one shared component on two surfaces. Extension inside the established world (DESIGN.md glass system); no new identity. Modes: Team Directory = Operate (find a colleague, write, save a contact); home team section = Persuade (meet the people, open a profile, book a call).

Owner (22 Oct 2026): "create a good design for every card. make it highly interactive and animated"; chose "spotlight + quick view: cursor light and tilt on the card, glowing photo ring, and clicking opens a slide-in panel with the full profile, skills and contact. Apply to the Team Directory and the home team cards."

## Direction contract
THESIS: a person, not a record. Each card is a lit glass portrait the cursor illuminates; one click brings the whole person to the side of the page without leaving it. Refuses the HR-sheet / field-list card.
OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md glass on the blue grid; photo in a blue conic ring that wakes and turns under the cursor; a soft blue spotlight following the pointer across the card and a light rim on its edge; quick view as a frosted panel sliding in from the right (bottom sheet on phones) over a blurred page.
STORY: scan faces and roles; hover to light one up; click to see the full bio, stats, every skill and (directory only) email and vCard; step to the next person with arrows; open the full profile page or book a call.
FIRST VIEWPORT: grid of portrait cards: 150px photo ring centred, name, role, office; home adds the one-line summary and stats; directory adds email and top skills with Email / Save contact actions.
FORM: extension of the established surface; no concept roll (impeccable new-work §3 "Extend an existing surface").
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Constraints: no email or phone on indexed pages (home quick view has none); light only; no eyebrows; reduced motion = no tilt, no ring spin, panel fades.
