# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** owners, finance managers and accountants of Philippine SMEs and growing companies who must meet BIR requirements (CAS/CBA books, 2307/2306, SLSP, SAWT, QAP, 2550Q, e-invoicing for covered taxpayers by 31 December 2026) and are outgrowing spreadsheets or a legacy accounting system. They arrive worried about deadlines, penalties and audits, and want someone who already knows the rules to set it up.
- **Secondary:** Singapore companies (GST, InvoiceNow/Peppol) and regional groups (10+ countries) that need Odoo ERP, enterprise AI or a website.

## Product Purpose

technext.asia is TechNext Pte. Ltd.'s company website. It positions TechNext as a trusted expert adviser for ERP and government compliance and turns visitors into a **discovery call with an adviser** (Odoo booking calendar). Secondary routes: the inquiry form, the BIR adviser call (/bir-adviser-call), the quotation builder, the Services Catalogue. The free website offer ended on 28 Oct 2026; /get-started and /free-website-with-odoo redirect to /bir-adviser-call.

## Positioning

- Trusted expert adviser for ERP and compliance: we advise first, then our own experts implement (Singapore HQ, consulting/development hub in Taguig City, AI engineers in Ho Chi Minh City). Not just business vision: implementation, support after go-live, dedicated support on your team.
- BIR compliance handled in your Odoo is the lead message for the Philippines.
- Already delivered similar projects; can show the work.
- Website and brand design are services on quotation (no free website since 28 Oct 2026); the client always pays their own Odoo subscription.

## Operating Context

- Static site generated by Python (`_src/build.py`, pages in `_src/pages/`, data in `_src/sitedata.py`), deployed on Vercel from `master` of TechNextSG/TechNext-Website. Plain HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, no framework or bundler. CSP hashes inline scripts.
- Practices: Odoo ERP, Enterprise AI, Websites & Marketing (Technology practice removed 21 Oct 2026). Industries: medical, travel, retail, ecommerce, construction, F&B, manufacturing, health & wellness.
- Leads flow to Odoo CRM; booking via technext.odoo.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No pricing anywhere. Odoo is never "free"; only TechNext's design/setup labour can be.
- Never claim "BIR-accredited", "BIR-certified" or "BIR-compliant" software (BIR advisory 14 Sep 2026: no provider accredited). Say "set up for BIR requirements".
- No WhatsApp links or phone numbers on the site.
- Light theme only.
- Removed pages stay removed (offices, Technology, RAG page, careers, events, life, advisers); their redirects stay.
- Team profiles on the home page (22 Oct 2026): five real people from the owner's CVs and notes (`_src/advisors.py`): name, role, office, bio, stats and grouped skills. No company or client names (owner: "remove company worked on"), no contact details, no pronouns in bios; initials until the owner sends photos.

## Brand Commitments

- Name TechNext, plane logo; British spelling, "adviser". Voice: solution provider and trusted expert adviser; lead verbs advise/recommend/deliver; "You don't need to hire your own team." Avoid "world-class", "leading", "one-stop shop", "vendor", "agency", "reseller".
- Odoo app icons are the official flat Odoo style; no icon tiles or backdrops behind icons.
- Nexi, the robot mascot, and "Nexi Explains" series exist on the site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Odoo Ready Partner (Odoo partner directory listing); clients in 10+ countries; 11+ enterprise clients; 3 offices; 4 AI disciplines.
- Named clients (owner-approved 21 Oct 2026, delivered/in progress only): `_src/sitedata.py` CLIENTS (11).
- BIR sample reports with fictitious data (`assets/docs/technext-sample-bir-reports.pdf`), BIR articles, Services Catalogue (50 pp).
- **Absent, never fabricate:** testimonials, result metrics, case-study outcomes, team photos (pending), accreditation claims.

## Product Principles

1. BIR first for the Philippines: name the pain, show the fix in Odoo, cite the rule.
2. Advice before software: every page leads to a conversation with an adviser.
3. Proof, not promises: show real work, real screens, real clients; nothing invented.
4. One team end to end: the people who scope it build it and support it.
