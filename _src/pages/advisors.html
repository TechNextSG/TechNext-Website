why an adviser

Most ERP projects go wrong before the software is opened.

They go wrong when nobody asks how the business actually runs: who approves a discount, when stock is counted, which report the auditor wants. Our advisers start there. common questions

Working with an adviser, answered.

Is the adviser the same person who sets up Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. The adviser who maps your process leads the configuration with our consultants in Taguig City, trains your key users and stays your contact after go-live.

Do we need to decide on Odoo before we talk? {{icon:chevron}}

No. The first conversation is about how your business runs. If Odoo is not the right fit for a part of it, the adviser will tell you.

We already run Odoo. Can an adviser still help? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Advisers review an existing set-up, find where people work around the system, and recommend what to reconfigure, retrain or add. See Odoo support.

Which industries do your advisers know? {{icon:chevron}}

Medical, travel, retail, ecommerce, construction, F&B, manufacturing and health & wellness. Each has its own page under Industries.

Where are the advisers based? {{icon:chevron}}

Our headquarters is in Singapore, where we also work on-site. Our main consulting hub is in Taguig City, Metro Manila, and our AI engineers are in Ho Chi Minh City. We advise clients in 10+ countries online.

How do we book a first conversation? {{icon:chevron}}

Use Let's talk to pick a time, message us on WhatsApp, or email sales@technext.asia with a line on what slows your team down.
