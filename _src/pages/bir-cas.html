A Computerized Accounting System (CAS) records your transactions, generates your invoices and produces your books of accounts and BIR reports. Large taxpayers must use one; other businesses may choose a CAS or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA). Since RMC 5-2021 there is no Permit to Use: your Revenue District Office (RDO) issues an Acknowledgement Certificate within three working days of a complete application and evaluates the system afterwards. TechNext sets Odoo up as your CAS or CBA, prepares the sample printouts and audit trail your application needs, and plans the yearly ORUS books and the e-invoicing permit with you.

BIR notice: no software is BIR-accredited

The BIR registers your business's use of a system; it does not approve software brands. In a public advisory dated 14 September 2026 it said it has not accredited, certified or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider, and warned against claims such as "BIR-accredited". RMC 110-2026 (12 October 2026) sets the accreditation rules for e-invoicing service providers; no list had been published when we wrote this. We set Odoo up for BIR requirements and prepare your samples. The registration decision is the BIR's.

BIR CAS registration, answered.

The questions Philippine finance teams ask us before an Odoo project.

What is a BIR Computerized Accounting System (CAS)? {{icon:chevron}}

A CAS is a computerised system that records a business's transactions, generates its invoices and produces its books of accounts and financial reports. Under RMO 9-2021 a business registers its CAS with the BIR at its Revenue District Office (RDO) or Large Taxpayers office. If the system issues invoices by hand, it counts as Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) instead.

Is there BIR-accredited accounting software? {{icon:chevron}}

No. The BIR registers each taxpayer's use of a system, not a software brand. In its public advisory of 14 September 2026 it said it has not accredited any e-invoicing service provider and warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS or CBA and receives an Acknowledgement Certificate.

Do I still need a Permit to Use (PTU)? {{icon:chevron}}

No. RMC 5-2021 replaced the Permit to Use with registration and an Acknowledgement Certificate (AC). Existing PTUs stay valid unless revoked or the system has a major enhancement.

How long does CAS registration take? {{icon:chevron}}

The RDO issues the Acknowledgement Certificate within three working days of a complete application. There is no demonstration before you start: the RDO evaluates the system afterwards and can revoke the certificate if it finds gaps, which is why we check the requirements before you file.

Can Odoo Online or Odoo.sh be registered as a CAS? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. RMO 9-2021 covers cloud and third-party systems. You and the provider sign a joint sworn statement that the system cannot suppress sales, and the registration is cancelled if the provider contract ends.

Does my Acknowledgement Certificate let me issue e-invoices? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Under RMC 98-2026 the AC authorises the system but not e-invoicing. Covered taxpayers also need a Permit to Issue (PTI), then EIS Certification within six months, and the covered groups must issue e-invoices by 31 December 2026.

Do I need to re-register after upgrading Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

A version change that affects the financial side counts as a major enhancement, which needs an updated registration before you use it. Minor changes, such as screen changes or bug fixes, need only a written notice. We plan the update with your RDO alongside the upgrade.

Does TechNext file the CAS application for us? {{icon:chevron}}

Your business files at its RDO or Large Taxpayers office, in person or by email. We set up the system, prepare the sample printouts, the audit trail and the system description with you, and stay on call for the RDO's questions. The registration decision is the BIR's.
