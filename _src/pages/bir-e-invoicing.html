How RMC 98-2026 shapes the set-up.

RMC 98-2026, issued on 22 September 2026, sets the working rules. Each one maps to a configuration decision we make with you.

Read the BIR rules for your accounting system {{icon:arrow}}
  1. 1
    A Permit to Issue (PTI)

    You need a PTI Electronic Invoice before you issue any e-invoice.

  2. 2
    EIS Certification

    Completed within six months of getting the PTI.

  3. 3
    A system that can transmit

    Your system must be able to send the required sales data to the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System; sales reporting itself starts once the BIR issues its rules.

  4. 4
    No edits after issue

    An issued e-invoice cannot be deleted or changed. A credit note covers a decrease; a new e-invoice covers an increase.

  5. 5
    A plan for downtime

    If the system is down, issue a BIR-authorised manual invoice, then replace it with an e-invoice that references it.

  6. 6
    Every branch

    Head office and all branches issue e-invoices; different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs.

BIR e-invoicing, answered.

See how invoices get issued and sent {{icon:arrow}}
What has to be working by 31 December 2026? {{icon:chevron}}

Covered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026; RR 26-2025 set the date and RMC 98-2026 kept it. On the system side that means invoices numbered per branch series, locked once issued, credit-note and downtime flows in place, and a route to transmit sales data to the EIS, which standard Odoo does not include and we assess and quote separately.

Who confirms whether we are covered? {{icon:chevron}}

Your accountant or RDO, never TechNext. Micro taxpayers are exempt from mandatory BIR e-invoicing, e-commerce coverage applies to sellers classed as small, medium or large, and the BIR Commissioner can require other taxpayers to comply. We map your invoice flows with you once coverage is confirmed.

How do you prepare for the Permit to Issue (PTI)? {{icon:chevron}}

You apply for the PTI Electronic Invoice before issuing any e-invoice, then complete EIS Certification within six months of the PTI. We prepare the process flow and sample invoices from your Odoo with you; the permit decision is the BIR's.

Is Odoo BIR compliant? {{icon:chevron}}

No accounting software is certified as BIR compliant: the BIR does not accredit accounting software brands, it has not accredited or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider (BIR advisory, 14 September 2026), and the permit is issued to your business for the system you register. Odoo 20's Philippine localisation covers invoices set up for BIR and CAS requirements, a Book of Accounts, 2306 and 2307 certificates, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and 2550Q. Its release notes do not cover an EIS connection, and TechNext has not built one; we assess what one needs and plan it as separate work. You file your CAS and e-invoicing applications with the BIR yourself. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Can we fix an e-invoice after it is issued? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Issued e-invoices cannot be deleted or changed. Use a credit note for a decrease and a new e-invoice for an increase; we set both flows up in your system before go-live.

How are our branches handled? {{icon:chevron}}

BIR e-invoicing covers head office and all branches. Each branch runs its own invoice series in one database, and branches on different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs. See BIR rules for multi-branch businesses.
