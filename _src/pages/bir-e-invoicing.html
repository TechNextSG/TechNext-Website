The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) requires covered taxpayers to issue compliant electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026. Covered now: e-commerce sellers classed as small, medium or large, Large Taxpayers Service accounts, large taxpayers, and businesses on a Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) with invoicing software. Micro taxpayers are exempt. You need a Permit to Issue first, then EIS Certification within six months. BIR rules for your accounting system {{icon:arrow}}
  1. 1
    A Permit to Issue (PTI)

    You need a PTI Electronic Invoice before you issue any e-invoice.

  2. 2
    EIS Certification

    Completed within six months of getting the PTI.

  3. 3
    A system that transmits

    Your system must send the required sales data to the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System.

  4. 4
    No edits after issue

    An issued e-invoice cannot be deleted or changed. A credit note covers a decrease; a new e-invoice covers an increase.

  5. 5
    A plan for downtime

    If the system is down, issue a BIR-authorised manual invoice, then replace it with an e-invoice that references it.

  6. 6
    Every branch

    Head office and all branches issue e-invoices; different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs.

BIR e-invoicing, answered.

What is the BIR e-invoicing deadline? {{icon:chevron}}

Covered taxpayers must issue compliant electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RR 26-2025 set the date and RMC 98-2026 kept it.

Are micro taxpayers covered? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Micro taxpayers are exempt from the mandatory e-invoicing requirement, and e-commerce coverage applies to sellers classed as small, medium or large.

What is a Permit to Issue (PTI)? {{icon:chevron}}

The BIR permit you need before issuing any e-invoice. EIS Certification must then be completed within six months of the PTI.

Is Odoo BIR compliant? {{icon:chevron}}

Odoo 20's Philippine localisation covers invoices set up for BIR and CAS requirements, a Book of Accounts, 2306 and 2307 certificates, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and 2550Q. Its release notes do not cover the EIS connection, so TechNext builds and tests that as part of your project. Your CAS and e-invoicing applications are still filed with the BIR. The BIR has not accredited or endorsed any software or e-invoicing provider (BIR advisory, 14 September 2026), so treat any "BIR-accredited" claim with care: the permit is issued to your business for the system you register.

Can we fix an e-invoice after it is issued? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Issued e-invoices cannot be deleted or changed. Use a credit note for a decrease and a new e-invoice for an increase; we set both flows up in Odoo before go-live.

Does the rule cover our branches? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Head office and all branches must issue e-invoices, and branches on different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs.
