Key takeaways

  • SAWT = tax withheld from you by customers (from the 2307s you receive). QAP = tax you withheld from suppliers.
  • The QAP is attached to the 1601-EQ and 1601-FQ and is due with them, the last day of the month after the quarter (RMC 55-2026). The SAWT goes with the return where you claim the credit.
  • Files are validated by the BIR's Alphalist Data Entry and Validation Module (version 7.4 under RMC 15-2025) and sent through eSubmission.
  • Odoo exports both as DAT files compatible with the BIR Alphalist module, using the ATC codes on your withholding taxes.

Withholding tax creates paperwork on both sides of every transaction. When a customer withholds tax from your invoice, you collect a 2307 and claim the credit. When you withhold from a supplier, you remit it and issue the 2307. The BIR checks both sides through two alphalists. This guide explains each one and how Odoo produces them from your entries.

RMC 55-2026 (26 May 2026) reminds withholding agents that the alphalist is an attachment to the withholding return, so it shares that return's deadline. The same circular lists the monthly alphalist for 1600-VT and 1600-PT (10th of the following month), the annual alphalist for compensation and final tax (31 January) and the annual 1604-E alphalist (1 March).

How the files are submitted

  • Electronic only. RR 1-2014 requires all withholding agents, regardless of the number of payees, to submit alphalists electronically, and RMC 5-2014 confirmed that CDs, DVDs and USB drives are no longer accepted.
  • Validated first. The BIR's Alphalist Data Entry and Validation Module checks the file. RMC 15-2025 released version 7.4, with new alphanumeric tax codes and updated rates.
  • A fixed file name. Under RMC 15-2025 Annex B (the same convention as RMC 7-2021), monthly, quarterly and annual alphalist files of payees are named TIN + branch code + return period + form type, ending in .DAT. For a 1601-EQ, the period is month and year.
  • Proof of submission. The BIR replies by email with a success or failure notice. Keep it with the return.

Why the BIR rejects alphalists

RMC 5-2014 lists the conditions for an alphalist to count as received. The common failures are:

  • Dummy or missing TINs, such as 000-000-000-000.
  • Lumped entries like "Various payees" or "Others" instead of named payees.
  • Special characters such as ñ, *, ? and & in names.
  • Old module versions that no longer match the current file structure.
  • Partial amendments. A corrected list must be complete, not only the changed rows.

The stakes are real. Under RR 1-2014, a list that does not follow the prescribed format and fails to upload is treated as not received, and the related expenses do not qualify as deductions. When the BIR sends a failure notice, RMC 5-2014 gives you five days to resubmit a corrected, complete list.

How Odoo builds SAWT and QAP

Odoo's Philippine localisation produces both from the withholding taxes on your posted entries:

  • SAWT comes from customer invoices with sales withholding taxes. QAP comes from vendor bills with purchase withholding taxes.
  • The report is under Accounting > Reporting > Tax Report, as SAWT & QAP (PH).
  • Export SAWT & QAP creates a DAT file compatible with the BIR Alpha module. You choose what the file is attached to (for example, 1601-EQ). Odoo exports one month at a time, as its documentation advises. The BIR treats the QAP as a quarterly attachment (RMC 15-2025 Annex B), so check in the current validation module whether to combine the three months, and file it by the 1601-EQ deadline.
  • Every withholding tax needs its ATC code, set on the tax's Philippines tab. Without it, the 2307, SAWT and QAP cannot be built. See setting up withholding tax and ATC codes.

The BIR's own module remains the final check. Run Odoo's DAT files through it before you submit.

A quarter-end checklist

  1. Collect every 2307 from customers and match each to its invoice before you build the SAWT.
  2. Check that each withholding tax has the right ATC and that each partner has a valid TIN.
  3. Export the DAT files from Odoo and validate them in the current BIR module.
  4. Reconcile: QAP totals against the tax remitted in your 0619-E and 1601-EQ, SAWT totals against the credits you claim.
  5. File, then lock the period in Odoo.

Common questions

What is the difference between SAWT and QAP? {{icon:chevron}}

The SAWT lists tax that customers withheld from you, built from the 2307s you received. The QAP lists tax you withheld from your suppliers, attached to your 1601-EQ or 1601-FQ.

When is the QAP due? {{icon:chevron}}

With the 1601-EQ or 1601-FQ, on the last day of the month after the quarter closes, as RMC 55-2026 restates.

What happens if my alphalist fails validation? {{icon:chevron}}

The BIR emails the reasons. Under RMC 5-2014 you resubmit a corrected, complete list within five days. A list that never uploads is treated as not received, which can disallow the related expenses.

Does Odoo create SAWT and QAP files? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. The Philippine localisation exports both as DAT files compatible with the BIR Alphalist module, from the withholding taxes on your invoices and bills.
