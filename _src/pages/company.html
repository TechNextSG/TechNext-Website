why odoo, and why a partner

One system is easier to run than five.

Most growing companies run sales in one tool, stock in another and accounts in a third, joined by spreadsheets. Odoo puts them in one database. An Odoo Partner implements it with Odoo's methodology and direct access to Odoo's partner resources — so you get the standard product configured well, not a bespoke system nobody else can maintain.
