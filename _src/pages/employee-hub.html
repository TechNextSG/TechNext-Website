what a 201 holds

The 201 file, explained.

A 201 file is the personnel record HR keeps for each employee, from the day they are hired to the day they leave. In the Philippines it usually holds the documents on the right.

Keep yours current. Tell HR when your address, civil status, dependents, contact numbers or bank details change, so your sheet and your file stay the same.

201 · Personnel file

  1. Employee information sheetPersonal details, position, office, work email, emergency contact
  2. Government numbersTIN, SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG
  3. Pre-employment documentsBirth certificate, NBI clearance, ID photos, diploma or transcript
  4. Employment recordsJob offer, contract, job description and any changes to them
  5. Previous employmentCertificate of employment and BIR Form 2316 from the last employer
  6. Performance and trainingEvaluations, certificates and trainings attended
