what's on a card

Anatomy of a TechNext card.

Every card is built the same way, so a client can find a name, a title and an email at a glance.

  1. The logoThe TechNext plane and wordmark, top left.
  2. TechNext IDThe same blue ID everyone wears, with the person's photo or initials.
  3. Name and positionAs listed on this page, in navy.
  4. Work emailOne tap to write; work details only.
  5. Contact codeA space for a scannable code (coming soon). Until then, the vCard button saves the contact.
  6. Our officesSingapore HQ, Taguig City and Ho Chi Minh City, on every back.
