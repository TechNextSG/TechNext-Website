What does the BIR ask for?

RMC 98-2026 kept the 31 December 2026 date and set the working rules. These are the ones that shape your Odoo set-up.

  1. 1

    Permit to Issue (PTI)

    Needed before you issue any e-invoice.

  2. 2

    EIS Certification

    Completed within six months of getting the PTI.

  3. 3

    A system that transmits

    Your system sends the required sales data to the BIR's EIS.

  4. 4

    No edits after issue

    A credit note covers a decrease; a new e-invoice covers an increase.

  5. 5

    A plan for downtime

    A BIR-authorised manual invoice, replaced later by an e-invoice that references it.

  6. 6

    Every branch

    Head office and all branches; different systems may need separate PTIs.

Who must comply by 31 December 2026

  • {{icon:check}} Small, medium and large taxpayers selling through e-commerce
  • {{icon:check}} Taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Service, and large taxpayers under RA 11976
  • {{icon:check}} Businesses on CAS or CBA with e-invoicing or other invoicing software

Micro taxpayers are exempt. Not sure which group you are in? We check it with your accountant or Revenue District Office during discovery.

A word of caution

The BIR said on 14 September 2026 that it has not accredited or endorsed any software or e-invoicing provider, so we don't claim to be "BIR-accredited". We set up your Odoo for the BIR's requirements, build and test the link, and support your PTI application.

BIR and the offer, answered.

What is the BIR e-invoicing deadline? {{icon:chevron}}

Covered taxpayers must issue compliant electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RR 26-2025 set the date and RMC 98-2026 kept it.

Is TechNext BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

No, and the BIR has said it has not accredited any e-invoicing provider. What we do is set up your Odoo for the BIR's requirements, build and test the e-invoicing link, and prepare the process flow and sample outputs for your PTI application.

Can Odoo produce our 2307s, SLSP, SAWT and QAP? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Odoo 20's Philippine localisation generates 2306 and 2307 certificates from vendor bills, and SLSP, SAWT and QAP with DAT exports, from the same entries as your books.

We already have a CAS permit for our current system. What happens? {{icon:chevron}}

A new accounting system usually needs its own BIR registration. Check the details with your accountant; we prepare the process flow and sample outputs straight from your Odoo, so the application reflects the system you will actually use.

Is the website really free? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Start your Odoo with TechNext and your website and brand design come with it, set up by the same team. Your Odoo subscription stays in your company's name.

We already have a website. Can we still join? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. We can rebuild it in Odoo with your current content, or keep your site and connect its forms to Odoo. We'll recommend the better option on the call, and say why.

Who can claim it? {{icon:chevron}}

Companies in the Philippines, Singapore and Vietnam that run their Odoo with TechNext. In Singapore we set up GST and InvoiceNow instead of BIR. We confirm the details on the first call.
