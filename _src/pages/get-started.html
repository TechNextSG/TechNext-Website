Questions we hear first

Do we have to replace our current system? {{icon:chevron}}

No. TechNext adds Odoo as a plug-in in your system: you keep your current accounting software, POS or in-house system, and Odoo produces the books and BIR forms from the data we bring in. Later, Odoo can grow app by app.

How much does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

We keep it affordable for SMEs: you can start with Odoo Accounting only. After the meeting you get a written quote for your scope before any work starts. Odoo's subscription is separate and is paid to Odoo.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

No accounting software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS or CBA at its Revenue District Office. TechNext is not affiliated with or acting for the BIR. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Can TechNext set Odoo up for our BIR CAS registration? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes, on the Odoo side. We set Odoo up for your Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) application and prepare the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail printout with you. You file at your RDO; the decision is the BIR's. How BIR CAS set-up works.

How long does it take? {{icon:chevron}}

As quick as 5 working days for the set-up, once your process is mapped and your data is ready. In a free 15 to 20 minute meeting a BIR Compliance Expert looks at your books and gives you your 5 Day plan. Bigger scopes run as extra sprints, written into your quote. Your RDO sets its own timeline for the CAS registration. If you need your books ready for ORUS registration by 30 January 2027, tell us early.

It is our own way of fitting a BIR CAS set-up into days, not months, run by our BIR Compliance Experts and accounting workflow experts. We walk you through it in your free planning meeting. See the method.
