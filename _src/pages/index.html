Questions we hear first

Do we have to replace our current system? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Most clients keep the system they run today, such as QuickBooks, Xero, SAP Business One, a POS or an in-house system, and use Odoo for the books and BIR forms. We connect the two so the data you need lands in Odoo. If you later want more of your business in Odoo, it grows app by app.

How much does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

We keep it affordable for SMEs: you can start with Odoo Accounting only. After the call you get a fixed, written quote for your scope before any work starts. Odoo's subscription is separate and is paid to Odoo.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

No software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS or CBA at its Revenue District Office. TechNext is not affiliated with or acting for the BIR. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Can TechNext set Odoo up for our BIR CAS registration? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes, on the Odoo side. We set Odoo up for your Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) application and prepare the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail printout with you. You file at your RDO; the decision is the BIR's. How BIR CAS set-up works.
