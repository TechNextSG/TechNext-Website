    What does TechNext do? {{icon:chevron}}

    TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner in Singapore with its main team in Taguig City. Our own specialists plug Odoo into the system a company already runs and look after the whole project: discovery, set-up, data migration, training and support.

    Do we have to replace our current system? {{icon:chevron}}

    No. TechNext adds Odoo as a plug-in in your system: you keep your current accounting software, ERP, POS or in-house system, and Odoo works from the data we bring in. Later, Odoo can grow app by app.

    Who does the work? {{icon:chevron}}

    TechNext's own consultants and developers, based in Taguig City and Singapore. The expert in your first meeting works on your set-up, so nothing is lost in a hand-off. Meet the experts.

    How does a project start? {{icon:chevron}}

    With a 15 to 20 minute online meeting. A TechNext expert looks at how your business runs today and what Odoo would plug into. You then get a written recommendation and a quote for that scope before any work starts.

    How much does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    It depends on your scope. You can start with Odoo Accounting only, and you get a written quote before any work starts. Odoo's own subscription is separate and is paid to Odoo.

    Where is TechNext based? {{icon:chevron}}

    TechNext's headquarters is at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore. Our main consulting and development hub is in Taguig City, Metro Manila, and our engineering hub is in Ho Chi Minh City. We work with clients in 25+ countries.
