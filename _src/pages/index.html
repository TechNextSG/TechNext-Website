What does TechNext do? {{icon:chevron}}

TechNext implements Odoo ERP end to end (discovery, configuration, data migration, training, integration and support), builds enterprise AI, websites and social media, and delivers IoT, app development and network services for growing companies.

Is TechNext an official Odoo partner? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory.

Where is TechNext based? {{icon:chevron}}

Our headquarters is at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261. Our main development and consulting hub is in Taguig City, Metro Manila, our AI engineering hub is in Ho Chi Minh City, and we serve clients in 10+ countries.

How much does an Odoo implementation cost? {{icon:chevron}}

There are two parts: Odoo licences, priced per user per month by Odoo, and the implementation work, which TechNext quotes in writing. The quote depends on the apps, number of users, data to migrate, integrations, custom modules and training.

Which Odoo apps should we start with? {{icon:chevron}}

Most companies start with Accounting, Sales and Inventory, the apps where money and stock move, and add the rest once the first phase is running.

Can we add Odoo apps later? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Every Odoo app shares the same database, so adding an app later needs no integration project; it uses the customers, products and accounts already in Odoo.

When was Odoo 20 released? {{icon:chevron}}

Odoo unveiled Odoo 20 at Odoo Experience 2026 at Brussels Expo, and published the release notes in September 2026.

What does TechNext need from us to start? {{icon:chevron}}

A short description of how your process runs today, a decision-maker for scope questions, and later the people who run sales, stock and finance for discovery and training.
