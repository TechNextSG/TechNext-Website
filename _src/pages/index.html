BIR, Odoo and TechNext, answered.

Who must issue BIR e-invoices by 31 December 2026? {{icon:chevron}}

Under RR 26-2025: e-commerce and internet sellers that are small, medium or large; taxpayers under the Large Taxpayers Service; large taxpayers under RA 11976; and businesses on a CAS or CBA with invoicing software. Micro taxpayers are exempt. Exporters, POS users and incentive-registered enterprises join later. Confirm your group with your accountant.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

No software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers your books and issues your Permit to Issue for the system you use, and its 14 September 2026 advisory says it has not accredited any e-invoicing provider. Your business can register Odoo as its computerised books and use it for its PTI application.

What does Odoo produce for the BIR? {{icon:chevron}}

Odoo's Philippine localisation produces the 2307 per payee, the SLSP, SAWT and QAP as DAT files for the BIR's tools, and the 2550Q VAT summary, from the invoices and bills you post. The connection to the BIR's EIS is project work that we build and test on a copy of your database.
