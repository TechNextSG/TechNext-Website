                  Seat and order: guests are seated at a table on the floor plan and their order is taken on the restaurant Point of Sale.

                  Kitchen: the order is split by product category into tickets for the grill, the fryer and the drinks station on the kitchen display, each with a timer that turns red when it runs late.

                  Serve and pay: when every ticket is ready the table is served; the guest pays by card or cash.

                  Recipes: each dish has a recipe (a bill of materials), so the paid order deducts its ingredients from stock and the food cost of each dish is known. Ingredients below their par level raise purchase orders to the supplier.

                  Close: closing the POS session posts sales by category, GST, card and cash payments, and the cost of the ingredients used into Accounting.

                  {{icon:check}} Sample menu, prices and recipes, time compressed. Stock can move when each order is paid or when the session closes, depending on your Point of Sale settings; stations, alert times and journals are set up per outlet in discovery.
