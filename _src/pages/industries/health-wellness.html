      Subscriptions and bookings

        Timetable: a week of yoga, Pilates, HIIT and spin classes, each with a capacity; a full class takes a waitlist, and a cancellation moves the next client up.

        Client record: the sample client's class bookings, 1:1 sessions paid when booked, front-desk retail sales and membership invoices, all on one record.

        Memberships: subscriptions renew and bill a saved card, upgrade or close with a reason; monthly recurring revenue is broken down into new, upgrades and churn, with a new members campaign as a scenario for the next three months.

        {{icon:check}} Sample studio, prices and figures; the campaign months are a scenario, not a forecast. Class capacity is standard in Odoo Appointments; a waitlist like this one is a small customisation we scope with you.
