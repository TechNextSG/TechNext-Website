            Structure: an office desk is made from a steel frame and a desk top, which are manufactured from their own bills of materials, and a fixing kit, a kit BoM of screws, cam locks and an Allen key.

            Cost roll-up: each component's quantity times its cost, plus each operation's minutes at its work centre's cost per hour, adds up level by level to the desk's unit cost of S$ 116.40 in this sample.

            Work centres: cutting, welding, painting, woodworking, assembly and packing, each with its minutes per unit and its load against the hours available this week.

            MRP: demand from sales orders, less stock, becomes manufacturing orders for the desk and its sub-assemblies; components short of stock raise purchase orders to their vendors, with each vendor's lead time.

            {{icon:check}} Sample data. Here the scrap allowance is added to the component's BoM quantity; actual scrap is recorded on the manufacturing order. Costing method, routes and lead times are agreed in discovery.
