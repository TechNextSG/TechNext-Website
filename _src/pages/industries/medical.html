      {{icon:search}}Lot traceability and recallClick a lot on the shelves

      Shelves: saline, gauze and hand sanitiser held in lots, each with an expiry date, an alert date 30 days before it and a removal date 7 days before it.

      Picking: FEFO reserves the lot with the earliest removal date first. FIFO reserves the lot received first, so a short-dated lot received later can reach its removal date on the shelf.

      Alerts and quarantine: the alert date raises an activity for the stock controller; at the removal date the lot moves to a quarantine location that is never reserved for picking.

      Recall: the traceability report follows one lot back to its supplier receipt and forward to every transfer, delivery and customer, so the remaining stock can be blocked, customers contacted and the lot returned to the supplier.

      {{icon:check}} Sample data and product settings. Odoo records the lots and dates, applies the removal strategy and raises the alert; quarantine moves, customer contact and returns are steps your team confirms. Clinical records stay in your medical record system.
