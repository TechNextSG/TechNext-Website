the board

Four steps, the same people all the way.

The people who map your processes are the people who train your team, connect your systems and answer the support tickets after go-live.

  1. Step 1{{icon:search}}

    Discovery

    We map how orders, stock and money move today, match each step to an Odoo app and write the scope down.

    Discovery {{icon:arrow}}
  2. Step 2{{icon:graduation}}

    Training

    Each team learns its own screens on a copy of your data, with a quick-reference guide for every task.

    Training {{icon:arrow}}
  3. Step 3{{icon:plug}}

    Integration

    Banks, payment gateways, online stores and the tools you keep are connected, so nobody re-types between systems.

    Integration {{icon:arrow}}
  4. Step 4{{icon:lifebuoy}}

    Support

    Fixes, month-end help, upgrades and small changes, handled by the team that configured your system.

    Support {{icon:arrow}}
