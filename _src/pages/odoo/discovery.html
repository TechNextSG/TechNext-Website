    Today, at a sample distributor: orders arrive on WhatsApp, by email and by phone; prices are copied from a spreadsheet into a quote document; discount approvals wait in the manager's inbox; the confirmed order is re-typed into an order log for the warehouse; delivery orders are handwritten and the signed copy comes back days later; invoices and supplier bills are keyed by hand into a separate accounting package; bank lines are ticked off a PDF; month-end reports are rebuilt in Excel.

    With Odoo: requests land in CRM; the quotation takes the customer's pricelist and shows stock on each line; an approval rule routes over-limit discounts to the manager; confirming the order creates the delivery; deliveries are validated by barcode; the invoice comes from delivered quantities with GST 9%; reordering rules raise purchase requests; bills are matched to the order and the receipt; the bank feed is reconciled by rules and reports read live.

    The method: interviews, process maps, app mapping, gaps flagged as configuration, integration, custom work or data, and a written scope with a quotation.

    Discovery, answered.

    Do we have to commit to a full implementation to do discovery? {{icon:chevron}}

    No. The scope and quotation are yours to act on, with us, with another Odoo partner, or in your own time. Discovery stands on its own.

    Who needs to be in the sessions? {{icon:chevron}}

    The people who do the work: finance, sales, warehouse and operations, plus whoever signs the scope off. We want the real process, not the org chart.

    On-site or remote? {{icon:chevron}}

    Either. We run sessions on-site in Singapore and remotely for teams in other locations.

    What if a step doesn't fit standard Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

    It gets flagged as configuration, integration or custom development, and priced separately, so you can decide whether it is worth building or whether the process should change instead.

    Will you touch our data or systems? {{icon:chevron}}

    We review what has to migrate and which systems stay, but nothing changes during discovery. It is a mapping exercise, not a cut-over.

    How much does discovery cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    On quotation, sized to the flows and teams in scope. Tell us what you run and we will come back with a number: request a quotation.
