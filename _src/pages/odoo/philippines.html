    BIR glossary, in plain English.

    The ten terms that come up in every Philippine accounting conversation. Each links to the guide that goes deeper.

    CAS
    Computerized Accounting System. An accounting system that produces your books and invoices electronically, registered with the BIR before you rely on it. How we set up your CAS
    CBA
    Computerized Books of Accounts. Your books are kept by computer but your invoices stay on BIR-printed or manual forms. Also registered with the BIR. CAS or CBA?
    PTI
    Permit to Issue (Electronic Invoice). The permit you plan for before issuing any e-invoice. Under RMC 98-2026 it comes first, with EIS certification within six months. E-invoicing guide
    EIS
    Electronic Invoicing System. The BIR's platform that receives e-invoices from covered taxpayers. Connecting Odoo to it is project work we plan with you. Deadline and steps
    ORUS
    Online Registration and Update System. The BIR portal where you register your books and update your registration. Registering in ORUS
    2307
    Certificate of creditable tax withheld at source. You give one to each payee you withhold from; they claim it against their income tax. 2307 in Odoo
    SLSP
    Summary List of Sales and Purchases. A listing of your sales and purchases with each counterparty's TIN, filed as a DAT file. SLSP guide
    SAWT
    Summary Alphalist of Withholding Taxes. The list of taxes your customers withheld from you, backed by the 2307s they gave you. SAWT and QAP
    QAP
    Quarterly Alphalist of Payees. The list of payees you withheld tax from during the quarter, with the amounts. SAWT and QAP
    2550Q
    Quarterly VAT return. Output VAT from your sales less input VAT from your purchases, for the quarter. 2550Q in Odoo

    Odoo in the Philippines, answered.

    Is TechNext an Odoo partner in the Philippines? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory, with its main development and consulting hub at Level 9, IP Center, Taguig City, Metro Manila.

    Is Odoo BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

    No, and no software is. Odoo 20's Philippine localisation covers the day-to-day BIR work: invoices set up for BIR and CAS requirements, a Book of Accounts for CAS and CBA, 2306 and 2307 certificates, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and 2550Q. It is not a BIR registration in itself: your CAS and e-invoicing applications are still filed with the BIR, and TechNext helps you prepare them from your own Odoo. The BIR has not accredited or endorsed any software or e-invoicing provider (BIR advisory, 14 September 2026), so treat any "BIR-accredited" claim with care: the permit is issued to your business for the system you register. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

    Can Odoo generate BIR Form 2307? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. Odoo 20 generates official BIR 2306 and 2307 withholding tax certificates as PDFs from vendor bills, and can send them to the vendor when a bill is paid.

    Does Odoo support Philippine payroll? {{icon:chevron}}

    Odoo 20 adds a basic Philippine payroll package with basic pay, benefits, taxes and overtime, plus Forms 1601-C, 2316 and 1604-C with Alphalist DAT exports, and automatic SSS and Pag-IBIG loan deductions.

    Does Odoo 20 support the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System (EIS)? {{icon:chevron}}

    The Odoo 20 release notes do not mention EIS. If your company is covered by it, TechNext plans the connection as a separate piece of work during discovery. Covered taxpayers must issue e-invoices by 31 December 2026: see BIR e-invoicing with Odoo.

    Can one Odoo database run our Philippine and Singapore companies? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. Odoo runs a company per country in one database, each with its own localisation, currencies and reports, with intercompany transactions between them.
