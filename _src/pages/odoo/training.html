common questions

Training, answered.

Is training on a demo or on our own system? {{icon:chevron}}

On a staging copy of your database, not a demo. People learn on your real products, customers and layouts, so nothing looks unfamiliar on go-live day.

On-site or remote? {{icon:chevron}}

Both. We run sessions on-site in Singapore and remotely for teams in other locations.

Who should attend? {{icon:chevron}}

Administrators first, then each team for its own screens. We train key users deeply so they can help train the rest.

What is UAT? {{icon:chevron}}

User acceptance testing: your team runs real tasks end-to-end before go-live, so questions and gaps surface while there is still time to fix them.

Can we train new hires later? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. The quick-reference guides let you onboard without us, and we can run refresher sessions whenever the team grows or Odoo changes.

How much does training cost? {{icon:chevron}}

On quotation, sized to the teams and sessions involved — request a quotation.
