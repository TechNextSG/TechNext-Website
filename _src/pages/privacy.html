How we collect, use and protect the personal data you send us through this website, and how cookies are used.

Who we are

This website is run by TechNext Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202699888G), 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261, with a team in Taguig City, Metro Manila. We decide how the personal data sent through this site is used. Questions about this policy go to our data protection contact at sales@technext.asia.

What we collect

Forms. When you send a form on this site (a meeting request, the Let's Talk panel, a landing page form or the BIR audit guide request) we collect what you enter: your name, company, work email, mobile number, the goal or topic you pick, and any message. Fields marked with an asterisk are required; the message is optional.

Meeting bookings. If you book a meeting, you do it on our booking page at technext.odoo.com, run on Odoo's appointment app. It collects your name, email, mobile number, company, the focus of the call and the time you choose.

Technical data. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security. Forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks your browser for signs of automated spam. We record which page a form was sent from and, if the visit came from an ad or campaign link, the campaign tags in that link.

We do not collect payment details, government ID numbers or tax returns through this website. Please do not send them in a form.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, set up the meeting you asked for, send the guide you asked for and, if you ask, prepare a written quote.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence and of any work we agree.
  • To measure which ads and pages bring enquiries, as described under Cookies and ad measurement.
  • To keep the website and our forms secure and free of spam.

We send you emails about your own request, such as a confirmation, a meeting reminder or the guide you asked for. We do not sell personal data, and we do not add you to marketing newsletters unless you agree; any newsletter carries an unsubscribe link.

Who processes it

Your details are recorded in our customer relationship system and booking app (Odoo, hosted by Odoo S.A.) and handled by our team, who reply from sales@technext.asia. Other providers process only what they need to deliver their service:

  • Vercel hosts this website and its form endpoint.
  • Cloudflare runs the Turnstile spam check on forms.
  • Google serves the fonts and, with your consent, Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement.
  • Meta measures our Facebook and Instagram ads, as described below.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) shows the official Odoo videos on some pages, only when you press play. Google Maps opens only when you follow a map link on our Company page.

These providers may store data outside your country, including in Singapore, the Philippines, the United States and the European Union. We use providers that protect personal data to a standard comparable to the PDPA and the Data Privacy Act.

Cookies and ad measurement

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, Google Ads to measure whether our ads lead to enquiries, and the Meta Pixel to do the same for our Facebook and Instagram ads. These tools set cookies (such as _ga, _gcl_au and _fbp) only after you choose Accept on the cookie notice. Until then Google Consent Mode keeps Google's cookies switched off and Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals, and the Meta Pixel does not run.

When you send a form, our server may tell Meta that an enquiry came in, with the ad click ID from the Facebook or Instagram ad link you followed (if any), so we can count which ad worked. If you chose Accept, the measurement also includes a one-way encoded (hashed) copy of the email, mobile number and name you entered, which Meta uses only to match the enquiry to an ad. If you chose Decline, no contact details are sent to Meta.

Accepting also sets first-party tn_ cookies that remember your choice and record your visit count, the pages you viewed and how you arrived (referrer and campaign tags), for up to a year. Choosing Decline keeps all of these off, and the site works the same either way. To change your mind, clear this site's cookies in your browser and the notice will appear again.

Nexi, the animated robot on our home page, runs in your browser. It sends nothing to us and only remembers whether you muted its sounds, in your browser's local storage (not a cookie).

Retention

We keep enquiry details and correspondence for as long as we need them to respond, to follow up on a meeting or quote you asked for, and to keep a business record, and then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your rights (Singapore PDPA)

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 you may ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to its use. Write to sales@technext.asia and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Philippine Data Privacy Act

If you are in the Philippines, or our Taguig City team handles your enquiry, we process your personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). We use only what you enter to reply to you, to prepare anything you ask for, to measure our ads as described above and to keep a record of our correspondence, on the basis of your consent. Your details are stored in Odoo and handled by our Singapore and Philippine teams under organisational, physical and technical safeguards.

You have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to its processing, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, to claim damages, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to sales@technext.asia, attention: Data Protection Officer, and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Security

The site runs only over HTTPS, forms are protected against spam and abuse, and access to enquiry records is limited to the people who need them. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive documents through a form.
