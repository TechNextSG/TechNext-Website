1. Who we are

This website is run by TechNext Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202699888G), 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261 ("TechNext", "we", "us"). We have offices in Taguig City, the Philippines, and Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. TechNext decides why and how the personal data described here is used, so we are the organisation responsible for it (the "personal information controller" in the Philippines and the "controller" in Vietnam).

This policy covers personal data collected through this website and our replies to you. When we deliver a project, the service agreement for that project sets out how we handle data in your systems on your behalf.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and, if you ask, prepare a quotation.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence and of any agreement we make with you.
  • To assess job applications.
  • To run, secure and improve the website, and, if you accept cookies, to measure visits and whether our ads lead to inquiries.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as tax and record-keeping rules.

We use your data only for these purposes, or for a new purpose we tell you about first and, where the law requires, ask your consent for. We do not sell personal data.

4. Consent and withdrawal

When you submit a form, email us or message us, you consent to us using your details to respond. Analytics and advertising cookies are used only after you press Accept on the cookie notice. In the Philippines and Vietnam we may also rely on other lawful grounds the law allows, such as taking steps towards a contract you asked for, or a legal obligation.

You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to our Data Protection Officer (section 17). We will tell you what withdrawing means for you (for example, we may be unable to continue preparing a quotation) and stop the processing within the time the law sets. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, and Google Ads to measure whether our advertising leads to inquiries. These tools set cookies (such as _ga and _gcl_au) only after you choose Accept on the cookie notice. Until then Google Consent Mode keeps them switched off and Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals.

Accepting also sets first-party tn_ cookies that remember your choice and record your visit count, the pages you viewed and how you arrived (referrer and campaign tags), for up to a year. Choosing Decline keeps all of these off, and the site works the same either way. To change your mind, clear this site's cookies in your browser and the notice will appear again.

The site also stores a few small preferences in your browser's local storage, not as cookies (for example whether you have already seen an intro animation, or whether you muted Nexi's sounds). They stay on your device.

Maps, videos and fonts

The office maps come from Google Maps. They load only when you choose Show the map, or on their own if you have accepted cookies; until then no request is made to Google Maps. Official Odoo videos come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Fonts are served by Google Fonts.

6. Nexi, our AI companion

Nexi, the AI companion on this page, answers inside your browser. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is gone when you leave the page.

7. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the website and reply to you, and only what each needs:

  • FormSubmit delivers form submissions to sales@technext.asia.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google provides Analytics, Ads measurement, Maps, YouTube and Fonts, as described in section 5.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries messages you send to our WhatsApp number.
  • JobStreet handles applications made through its listings.

Within TechNext, your inquiry may be handled by our team in Singapore, the Philippines or Vietnam. We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a regulator or a court.

8. Transfers between countries

Our team works across Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam, and some of our providers store data in other countries, including the United States. When personal data leaves the country where it was collected, we make sure the receiving party protects it to a standard comparable to the law of that country, through contracts, the provider's own legal commitments, or other measures the law recognises.

For data subject to Vietnamese law, we prepare and keep the cross-border transfer impact assessment the law requires and make it available to the authority on request. For data subject to Philippine law, we remain responsible for data we transfer and use contracts to secure comparable protection.

9. How we protect it

We use reasonable security arrangements: the website is served only over encrypted HTTPS, inquiry mailboxes are limited to the staff who need them, and our providers are chosen for their security practices. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending more information than your inquiry needs.

10. How long we keep it

We keep inquiry correspondence only as long as needed to respond and to keep a business record, then delete or anonymise it. Records linked to a contract are kept for the period that tax, accounting and company laws require. Cookie data lasts up to a year (section 5). Unsuccessful job applications are deleted once the role is filled unless you ask us to keep yours on file.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and learn how it has been used or disclosed in the past year;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • withdraw consent or object to processing;
  • delete, block or restrict processing of your data;
  • receive a copy in a portable format (data portability);
  • be told how your data is processed; and
  • complain to the data protection authority (section 14).

Write to our Data Protection Officer. We may need to confirm who you are before acting. We reply as soon as we can and within the time limits the law sets for your country. Some requests may be limited where the law allows, for example where data must be kept for a legal claim.

12. Data breaches

If a data breach happens, we act to contain it and assess it straight away. Where the law requires, we notify the regulator and the people affected:

  • Singapore: the PDPC within 3 calendar days of assessing that a breach is notifiable, and affected individuals where the breach is likely to cause them significant harm.
  • Philippines: the National Privacy Commission and affected data subjects within 72 hours of knowledge of a breach involving sensitive or identity-fraud-enabling data likely to cause serious harm.
  • Vietnam: the Ministry of Public Security's specialised data protection agency within 72 hours of discovering a breach, as the law requires.

13. Marketing and Do Not Call

We do not add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to. We do not send marketing calls, texts or faxes to Singapore numbers unless you have given clear consent or the number is not on the Do Not Call Registry, as the PDPA requires. You can opt out of any marketing message at any time by replying or writing to us.

14. Country sections

SGSingapore

We follow the PDPA's obligations on consent, purpose limitation, notification, access and correction, accuracy, protection, retention limitation, transfer limitation, data breach notification and accountability, and the Do Not Call provisions. We respond to access requests within 30 days, or tell you within that time when we will. You may complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission.

PHPhilippines

We process personal data according to the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality in the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its Implementing Rules and Regulations and the National Privacy Commission's issuances. You have the rights to be informed, to access, to object, to erasure or blocking, to rectification, to data portability, to damages and to file a complaint. We have designated a Data Protection Officer, and we will register our data processing systems with the NPC where its registration rules apply to us. You may complain to the National Privacy Commission.

VNVietnam

Since 1 January 2026 we follow the Law on Personal Data Protection (No. 91/2025/QH15) and Decree 356/2025/ND-CP, which replaced Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. We ask for clear, voluntary consent for each purpose, unless another legal basis applies. You have the rights to be informed, consent, withdraw consent, access, correct, delete, restrict and object, to complain and to claim compensation. We keep a personal data processing impact assessment and, where we transfer data abroad, a cross-border transfer impact assessment, and file them with the Ministry of Public Security as the law requires.
