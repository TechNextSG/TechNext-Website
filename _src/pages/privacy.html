Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the personal data you send us through this website, and how cookies are used.

TechNext Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202699888G) · Last updated {{YEAR}}

What we collect

When you use the inquiry or quotation forms on this site we collect the details you enter: name, company, work email, phone number, the topic or scope you select, and your message. The BIR check form on our free website page also asks for your role, country, industry, number of branches, gross annual sales bracket, the accounting system and website you use today, whether you are registered for CAS or CBA, your biggest BIR concern and a good time to call. The self-check on that page stays in your browser unless you add its answers to the form. We do not collect payment details on this website.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and, where you ask for one, prepare a quotation.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.
  • To operate and secure the website.

We do not sell personal data. We do not add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

Who processes it

Form submissions are recorded as an inquiry in our customer relationship system (Odoo CRM, hosted by Odoo) so our team can follow up, and we reply from sales@technext.asia. This website is hosted on Vercel. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. Each provider processes only what is needed to deliver its service.

Our Company page links to Google Maps for directions to each office. Nothing is loaded from Google Maps until you follow one of those links. Official Odoo videos on our app pages come from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.

Retention

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a business record, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you may ask to access or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent to its use. Write to sales@technext.asia and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Philippine Data Privacy Act

If you are in the Philippines, or our Taguig City team handles your enquiry, we process your personal information under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). We use only what you enter to reply to you, to prepare anything you ask for and to keep a record of our correspondence, on the basis of your consent. Your details are stored in Odoo CRM and handled by our Singapore and Philippine teams under organisational, physical and technical safeguards.

You have the right to be informed, to access your data, to object to its processing, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to data portability, to claim damages, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Write to sales@technext.asia and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also lodge a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use this site, and Google Ads to measure whether our advertising leads to enquiries. These tools set cookies (such as _ga and _gcl_au) only after you choose Accept on the cookie notice. Until then Google Consent Mode keeps them switched off and Google receives only anonymous, cookieless signals.

Accepting also sets first-party tn_ cookies that remember your choice and record your visit count, the pages you viewed and how you arrived (referrer and campaign tags), for up to a year. Choosing Decline keeps all of these off; the site works the same either way. To change your mind, clear this site's cookies in your browser and the notice will appear again.

Nexi, the AI companion on this page, answers inside your browser: what you type is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is gone when you leave the page. Nexi only remembers whether you muted its sounds, in your browser's local storage (not a cookie).
