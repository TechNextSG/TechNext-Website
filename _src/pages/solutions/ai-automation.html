AI workflow automation for vendor bills, from inbox to payment run.

A TechNext agent reads each bill, checks it against the purchase order and receipt in Odoo, and routes it by how sure it is: sure bills go to a one-click batch approval, the rest to a person who checks the fields. Nothing posts or pays without a person. Drag the confidence threshold and watch the split between the fast track and review change, switch the day, and click any bill in the trace to follow it.

      Bills in: the bills inbox, supplier uploads and scans. Read the bill: OCR extracts vendor, bill number, dates, PO reference, subtotal, GST and total, each with a confidence score.

      Validation rules: PO match, amount within tolerance of the PO and receipt, duplicate check, GST 9% check. A failed rule sends the bill to exceptions, where the buyer confirms it or AP rejects it.

      Confidence gate: at or above the threshold the bill joins a one-click batch approval, then posts to Odoo Accounting; below it, an AP clerk reviews the fields and the budget owner approves. Posted bills are scheduled for payment by their terms, and Finance releases the payment batch.

      questions

      What teams ask before they start.

      Will it act without us? {{icon:chevron}}

      No. Anything irreversible — a payment, a posting, a message to a customer — waits for a person to approve. The agent prepares the step; you release it.

      How do we know what it did? {{icon:chevron}}

      Every action is logged against the record it touched, and any step can be replayed or rolled back if something looks wrong.

      What can it connect to? {{icon:chevron}}

      Email, documents, spreadsheets, Odoo and your other systems, joined through APIs and connectors so the agent works across them in one run.

      Can we stop it quickly? {{icon:chevron}}

      Yes. There's a kill switch and a pause per workflow, and access is scoped so the agent only ever sees what its task needs.

      Does it need Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

      No. Agents work with the tools you already use, and connect to Odoo when you run it — reading and preparing records inside the ERP.

      What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

      Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the workflow and systems involved. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
