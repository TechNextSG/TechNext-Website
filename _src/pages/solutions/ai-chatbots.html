questions

What teams ask before they start.

Will it promise something it can't deliver? {{icon:chevron}}

No. It answers only from approved content and reads prices and dates from your records, so it won't invent a commitment. Anything binding waits for a person.

What channels does it run on? {{icon:chevron}}

A website widget styled to your brand, WhatsApp through the Business API, and in-app assistants — the same knowledge and hand-off rules on each.

Can it hand over to a real person? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. It passes the conversation, context and customer record to your team in Live Chat or WhatsApp, and escalates on request, sentiment or uncertainty.

Does it capture leads? {{icon:chevron}}

It asks qualifying questions, captures the details and creates the CRM lead with the full transcript attached, so your team picks up with context.

Which languages does it handle? {{icon:chevron}}

It answers in the customer's language while your content stays in one, so you maintain a single source and still serve people in theirs.

What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

Pricing is on quotation, scoped to your channels and content. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
