An AI chatbot from TechNext answers customers and staff from your own content, on your website, on WhatsApp or inside your apps. It can look up orders, bookings and invoices, qualify leads, and pass the conversation to a person with the full context when a question needs one.

WhatsApp, website chat and email arrive in one inbox. The bot understands the intent and details of each message and routes it: order status (answered from the sales order and its delivery in Odoo), quotation request (qualified, then a CRM lead for Sales), support issue (an approved knowledge answer, else a Helpdesk ticket), booking (free slots read and booked) or other (passed to a person).

When a customer asks for a person, or the bot has no approved answer, the conversation is handed to an agent with the transcript, intent, order and customer record attached, and a reply-time target starts. The meter shows how many conversations the bot answered against how many went to a person.

What teams ask before they start.

Will it promise something it can't deliver? {{icon:chevron}}

No. It answers only from approved content and reads prices and dates from your records, so it won't invent a commitment. Anything binding waits for a person.

What channels does it run on? {{icon:chevron}}

A website widget styled to your brand, WhatsApp through the Business API, and in-app assistants, with the same knowledge and hand-off rules on each.

Can it hand over to a real person? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. It passes the conversation, context and customer record to your team in Live Chat or WhatsApp, and escalates on request, sentiment or uncertainty.

Does it capture leads? {{icon:chevron}}

It asks qualifying questions, captures the details and creates the CRM lead with the full transcript attached, so your team picks up with context.

Which languages does it handle? {{icon:chevron}}

It answers in the customer's language while your content stays in one, so you maintain a single source and still serve people in theirs.

What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

Pricing is on quotation, scoped to your channels and content. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
