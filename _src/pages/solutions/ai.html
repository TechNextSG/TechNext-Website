questions

What teams ask before they start.

Which of the four should we start with? {{icon:chevron}}

Start with the task that removes the most manual hours each week. Tell us the work; we'll say which discipline fits and what a first version takes.

Will the AI make things up? {{icon:chevron}}

Answers are grounded in your own sources and cite them. When your documents don't cover a question, the system says so and can route it to a person rather than guess.

Do we have to be on Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

No. The knowledge assistants, automation agents and chatbots work with the tools you already use. AI inside Odoo is for teams already running the ERP.

Where does our data go? {{icon:chevron}}

It stays in your environment. Where data can't leave, we deploy private-cloud or on-premise models and choose hosting for your requirements.

Can a person stay in control? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Anything that moves money, commitments or customer data waits for human approval, and every action is logged against the record it touched.

What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the task and the data involved. Request a quotation and we'll come back with a written scope.
