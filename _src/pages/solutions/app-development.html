In one paragraph

App development is designing and building the software people use on a phone, tablet or browser to do a specific job: book, order, check in, report or approve. TechNext builds mobile apps, web apps and portals around one clear workflow, connects them to Odoo and your other systems through APIs so nothing is typed twice, and supports them after launch.

tap through a job

A field app that fills Odoo as the job happens.

A technician runs the whole visit from a phone: check in, work through the checklist, take photos and get the customer's signature. Each step lands on the job in Odoo as it happens, so the office sees progress live and the invoice is ready when the van leaves. Switch the platform and the job, or tap a step to jump to it.

    questions

    What teams ask before they start.

    Native or cross-platform? {{icon:chevron}}

    We choose per project: one cross-platform codebase when it serves iOS and Android well, native code when the app leans heavily on device features.

    Can the app work with Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. Apps can read and write Odoo records through its API, so orders, bookings, job reports and stock updates land in the ERP without retyping.

    Do you build web apps too? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes: portals, dashboards and internal tools that run in the browser, on their own or alongside a mobile app.

    How long does a first version take? {{icon:chevron}}

    It depends on the scope. Most teams start with one workflow, launch it, then add to it. The quotation comes with a timeline for the first version.

    What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the screens, platforms and integrations involved. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
