In one paragraph

Cyber security for a growing company means protecting accounts, devices, networks and data against the attacks that actually happen: phishing, stolen passwords, ransomware and unpatched systems. TechNext reviews your current setup, fixes the gaps in order of risk, such as multi-factor sign-in, updates, backups, access rights and firewall rules, helps your team recognise attacks, and helps you recover if something goes wrong.

questions

What teams ask before they start.

We are a small team. Do we need this? {{icon:chevron}}

Small companies are often targeted because they are easier. Most of the protection comes from a handful of basics done well: multi-factor sign-in, updates, tested backups and a team that recognises phishing.

Where do we start? {{icon:chevron}}

With a review. We look at your accounts, devices, network and backups, then give you a short list of fixes in order of risk.

Will it slow my team down? {{icon:chevron}}

It shouldn't. We pick protection people can live with, such as sign-in prompts on trusted devices, password managers and updates outside working hours.

What if we are attacked? {{icon:chevron}}

Contact us straight away. We help you contain it, restore from backup and close the gap that was used, then review what to change.

What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

Pricing is on quotation, scoped to your team size, systems and the support you want. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
