The group: a holding company in Singapore (SGD) owning companies in the Philippines (PHP), Vietnam (VND) and Malaysia (MYR), each with its own books on one Odoo database.

Intercompany: Vietnam sells goods to the Philippines, and the invoice creates the matching vendor bill in the Philippine company; Singapore lends to Vietnam and charges Malaysia a management fee.

Month-end: each company closes its books, figures are translated into SGD, intercompany revenue and cost, receivables and payables, the loan and its interest are eliminated, together with the profit still sitting in the buyer's stock, and the group P&L is produced.

How groups usually ask it.

Do all entities have to go live at once? {{icon:chevron}}

No. We implement a pilot entity first, then roll out company by company. Each entity goes live when ready.

How does consolidation work? {{icon:chevron}}

Group P&L, balance sheet and cash come from the same database, with eliminations done in Odoo, so there is no spreadsheet consolidation.

Can each country keep its own tax rules? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Every entity uses its own country localisation, and group reports roll up in one currency.

Who can see what? {{icon:chevron}}

People see their own company's data and only the group views you grant, with approval chains and audit trails.

Where does the group's data live? {{icon:chevron}}

Usually on Odoo.sh; on your own cloud where data residency requires it. We decide per region in discovery.

How is a group project priced? {{icon:chevron}}

By the entities, countries and integrations involved. Tell us how the group is structured and TechNext will return a phased, fixed written quote before any work starts.
