What Odoo for groups covers

Odoo for groups runs several companies, countries and currencies on one Odoo database. Inter-company transactions post automatically, each entity keeps its own books and local tax rules, and the board sees one consolidated set of numbers in the group currency. TechNext rolls it out in phases: one company first, then country by country.

Odoo multi-company consolidation, from local books to one group P&L.

A sample group on one Odoo database: a Singapore holding with companies in the Philippines, Vietnam and Malaysia, each keeping its own books and currency. An intercompany invoice creates the mirror bill in the other company, a group loan accrues interest, and at month-end the figures are translated into SGD and the intercompany lines are eliminated. Switch companies in and out of the group view or move the exchange rate, and the consolidated P&L TechNext sets up for you updates line by line.

The group: a holding company in Singapore (SGD) owning companies in the Philippines (PHP), Vietnam (VND) and Malaysia (MYR), each with its own books on one Odoo database.

Intercompany: Vietnam sells goods to the Philippines, and the invoice creates the matching vendor bill in the Philippine company; Singapore lends to Vietnam and charges Malaysia a management fee.

Month-end: each company closes its books, figures are translated into SGD, intercompany revenue and cost, receivables and payables, the loan and its interest are eliminated, together with the profit still sitting in the buyer's stock, and the group P&L is produced.

How groups usually ask it.

Do all entities have to go live at once? {{icon:chevron}}

No. We implement a pilot entity first, then roll the template out company by company. Each entity goes live when it's ready.

How does consolidation work? {{icon:chevron}}

Group P&L, balance sheet and cash are produced from the same database, with inter-company eliminations handled by the system. There's no separate month of spreadsheet consolidation.

Can each country keep its own tax rules? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Every entity uses its country's localisation for chart of accounts, tax and statutory reporting, while the group reports roll up in one currency.

Who can see what? {{icon:chevron}}

Access is controlled by granular rights and record rules per entity, with approval chains and audit trails. People see their company's data and only the group views you grant.

Where does the group's data live? {{icon:chevron}}

Usually on Odoo.sh, with staging and version control; on your own cloud where data residency requires it. We decide per region during discovery.

How is a group project priced? {{icon:chevron}}

By the entities, countries, integrations and custom work involved, so it's scoped and quoted rather than listed. Tell us how the group is structured and we'll come back with a phased quotation.
