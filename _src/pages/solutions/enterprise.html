questions we hear

How groups usually ask it.

Do all entities have to go live at once? {{icon:chevron}}

No. We implement a pilot entity first, then roll the template out company by company. Each entity goes live when it's ready.

How does consolidation work? {{icon:chevron}}

Group P&L, balance sheet and cash are produced from the same database, with inter-company eliminations handled by the system. There's no separate month of spreadsheet consolidation.

Can each country keep its own tax rules? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Every entity uses its country's localisation for chart of accounts, tax and statutory reporting, while the group reports roll up in one currency.

Who can see what? {{icon:chevron}}

Access is controlled by granular rights and record rules per entity, with approval chains and audit trails. People see their company's data and only the group views you grant.

Where does the group's data live? {{icon:chevron}}

Usually on Odoo.sh, with staging and version control; on your own cloud where data residency requires it. We decide per region during discovery.

How is a group project priced? {{icon:chevron}}

By the entities, countries, integrations and custom work involved, so it's scoped and quoted rather than listed. Tell us how the group is structured and we'll come back with a phased quotation.
