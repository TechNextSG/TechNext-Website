    Before you commit.

    Can Odoo handle BIR reporting? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes, with the Philippine localisation set up for your company. Odoo produces the 2307 per payee, the SLSP, SAWT and QAP as DAT files for the BIR's tools, and the 2550Q VAT summary, from the invoices and bills you post, and it can serve as your computerised books. Which reports you file is scoped in discovery. Odoo has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and TechNext has not built one. If you are covered, we assess the options with you and quote any connection separately.

    Is Odoo BIR-accredited? {{icon:chevron}}

    No software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers your books and issues your Permit to Issue for the system you use, and its 14 September 2026 advisory says it has not accredited any e-invoicing provider. We set Odoo up for BIR requirements; your business registers it as its books. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

    How long does an Odoo implementation take? {{icon:chevron}}

    It depends on scope: how many apps, how much data has to be migrated, how many entities, branches and integrations, and whether any custom module is needed. We set the timeline in discovery and write it into the quotation, so you plan against an agreed schedule rather than a guess.

    Which apps should we start with? {{icon:chevron}}

    Usually the ones causing the most manual work today. For many companies that is Accounting, Sales and Inventory, because they share the same products, partners and journals. We confirm the order in discovery.

    Can we keep some of our existing systems? {{icon:chevron}}

    Yes. If a system works and your team likes it, we connect it through a standard connector or the Odoo API instead of replacing it.

    What happens to our historical data? {{icon:chevron}}

    Master data (customers, vendors, products) comes across in full. For transactions we usually bring opening balances and open items, and keep the old system available for history. We agree the exact scope in discovery.

    Do you build custom features? {{icon:chevron}}

    Only when a real requirement can't be met by a standard feature. Custom work is built as a module on Odoo.sh, so it survives upgrades and stays version-controlled.

    Where will Odoo run? {{icon:chevron}}

    On Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or your own infrastructure. It is a scoping decision driven by whether you need custom modules and any data-residency rules, not a default.

    What does an implementation cost? {{icon:chevron}}

    Every implementation is scoped to the apps, data and integrations you need, so pricing is on quotation. Tell us what is manual today and we will come back with a plan.
