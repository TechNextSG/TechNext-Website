What social media management covers

TechNext's social media management runs your LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram on a monthly plan agreed with you: content produced and scheduled, comments and messages handled, and a one-page report each month. Strategy, design, short video and copy are included, and posts go out only after you approve them.

Social media management, from content calendar to qualified leads.

Each month TechNext plans the calendar with you, produces every post and sends it for your approval before it is scheduled. Run the sample month to watch posts move from draft to published, engagement build week by week, and enquiries arrive in Odoo CRM as leads. Drag a post to a new day, or select it to approve it, ask for changes or move it.
