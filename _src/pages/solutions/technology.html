In one paragraph

TechNext's technology services cover the devices, software and infrastructure around a company's business systems: IoT solutions that connect equipment and sites, mobile and web app development, and office networks and Wi-Fi. They are delivered by the same team that implements Odoo ERP and enterprise AI, so the pieces are planned together and the data ends up in one set of records.

Services working together
3

Freezer warming: the freezer sensor reads above its limit, the office network carries the reading, the supervisor's phone shows the alert and Odoo opens a quality check on the stock.

Machine stopped: the machine's runtime counter goes flat, the status reaches the server, the technician gets the job in the field app and Odoo opens a maintenance request.

Delivery arrived: the dock scale weighs the pallets, the weight reaches the server, the storekeeper confirms the count in the app and Odoo validates the receipt and updates the stock.

Without one of the services the information stops at that point: someone has to notice, walk over and type it in later.
