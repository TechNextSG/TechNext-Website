In one paragraph

TechNext's technology services cover the devices, software and infrastructure around a company's business systems: IoT solutions that connect equipment and sites, mobile and web app development, office networks and Wi-Fi, and cyber security. They are delivered by the same team that implements Odoo ERP and enterprise AI, so the pieces are planned together and the data ends up in one set of records.

questions

What teams ask before they start.

Do these services need Odoo? {{icon:chevron}}

No. Each one works on its own. When you run Odoo, we connect them so the data ends up in one place.

Can we start with just one? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Most companies start with one need, such as a new app, a network refresh or a security review, and add the rest when it makes sense.

Where is TechNext based? {{icon:chevron}}

TechNext is headquartered in Singapore, with teams in Taguig City and Ho Chi Minh City. Ask us about on-site work at your location.

What does it cost? {{icon:chevron}}

Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the services and sites involved. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
