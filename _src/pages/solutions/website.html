common questions

Good to know.

Can we edit the site ourselves afterwards? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. We build on platforms your team can update, and we hand over notes and a short walkthrough so you are not dependent on us for everyday changes.

Where is the site hosted, and who owns it? {{icon:chevron}}

Wherever you prefer — your own account or one we set up for you. The domain, the site and the accounts stay in your name.

Do you do SEO? {{icon:chevron}}

SEO basics are built in: clean titles and descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and fast, accessible pages. Ongoing SEO or content campaigns are a separate scope we can discuss.

Where do form inquiries go? {{icon:chevron}}

Straight to the inbox or CRM you name, so the right person sees every enquiry as it arrives.

Can you redesign our existing site? {{icon:chevron}}

Yes. Send us the current site or a blank page — either is a fine starting point, and we will tell you what to keep.

How is it priced? {{icon:chevron}}

Per scope, as a one-off build. We quote before any work begins, so you see the figure first. Get a quotation.
