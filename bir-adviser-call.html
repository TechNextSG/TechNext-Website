Do we need a CAS or a CBA?

RMO 9-2021 draws the line at invoices. If your system issues them, it is a CAS; if invoices are issued by hand and the system keeps your books, it is a CBA. Large taxpayers must use a CAS. We go through it with your accountant, and your RDO can confirm it.

Is TechNext BIR-accredited?

No, and no accounting software is. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. We set your system up for the BIR's requirements and prepare the sample outputs with you.

We already have a CAS permit for our current system. What happens?

A new accounting system usually needs its own BIR registration. Check the details with your accountant; we prepare the process flow and sample outputs from the system you will actually use.

Do we have to change our accounting software?

No. Your current software stays. We add a plug-in beside your current software, set up for your BIR books and reports, so nothing is swapped out while we work.
