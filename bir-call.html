What does the BIR ask for?

RMC 5-2021 lists what goes in a CAS or CBA application. These parts shape your Odoo set-up.

  1. 1

    A sworn statement

    A description of the system and the invoices and reports it produces; a joint one for a cloud system.

  2. 2

    Sample invoices

    Printed from the system you will actually use.

  3. 3

    Sample books and reports

    Your books of accounts and the other system reports.

  4. 4

    An audit trail printout

    From the system, showing who did what and when.

  5. 5

    The requirements checklist

    The functional and technical requirements in Annex B, signed.

  6. 6

    Your books, every year

    Computerised books registered online through ORUS within 30 days after year-end.

CAS or CBA: which one you register

  • Large taxpayers must use a CAS (RR 9-2009, restated in RMO 9-2021)
  • Your system issues the invoices: a Computerized Accounting System (CAS)
  • Invoices issued by hand, books kept in the system: Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA)

Odoo Online and Odoo.sh are registered too, with a joint sworn statement with the provider. Not sure which one you need? We go through it with your accountant during discovery; your Revenue District Office can confirm it.

A word of caution

The BIR registers your business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims, so we don't make them. We set up your Odoo for the BIR's requirements and prepare with you the samples for the CAS or CBA application you file at your RDO; the decision is the RDO's. TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner. We are not affiliated with, accredited by or acting for the BIR or any government agency, and we do not file or register on your behalf.

BIR and Odoo, answered.

Do we need a CAS or a CBA?

RMO 9-2021 draws the line at invoices. If your system issues them, it is a CAS; if invoices are issued by hand and the system keeps your books, it is a CBA. Large taxpayers must use a CAS. We go through it with your accountant on the first call, and your RDO can confirm it.

Is TechNext BIR-accredited?

No, and no accounting software is: the BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. What we do is set up your Odoo for the BIR's requirements and prepare with you the sample outputs and audit trail printout for the CAS or CBA application you file.

Can Odoo produce our 2307s, SLSP, SAWT and QAP?

Yes. Odoo 20's Philippine localisation generates 2306 and 2307 certificates from vendor bills, and SLSP, SAWT and QAP with DAT exports, from the same entries as your books.

We already have a CAS permit for our current system. What happens?

A new accounting system usually needs its own BIR registration. Check the details with your accountant; we prepare the process flow and sample outputs straight from your Odoo, so the application reflects the system you will actually use.

Who do you work with?

Companies in the Philippines, Singapore and Vietnam that run their Odoo with TechNext. In Singapore we set up GST instead, and work with the InvoiceNow Access Point you choose. We confirm the details on the first call.
