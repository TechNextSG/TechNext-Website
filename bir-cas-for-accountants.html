Before you book, answered.

Can the system produce our 2307s, SLSP, SAWT and QAP?

Yes. The plug-in we set up beside your current software generates 2306 and 2307 certificates from vendor bills, and SLSP, SAWT and QAP with DAT exports, from the same entries as your books.

Do we have to change our accounting software?

No. Your current software stays. We add a plug-in beside your current software, set up for your BIR books and reports, so nothing is swapped out while we work.

Who checks the set up?

You and your accountant. Our BIR Compliance Experts set up the taxes and reports; your accountant reviews the outputs before anything is filed.

Is TechNext linked to the BIR?

No. We are not affiliated with or acting for the BIR. You register and file yourself; we set your system up and prepare the sample outputs with you.

What does it cost?

It depends on your scope, so we don't publish a price. The meeting has no charge, and you get a written quote before any work starts.
