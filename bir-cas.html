BIR notice: no accounting software is BIR-accredited

The BIR registers your business's use of a system; it does not approve software brands. In a public advisory dated 14 September 2026 it said it has not accredited, certified or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider, and warned against claims such as "BIR-accredited". RMC 110-2026 (12 October 2026) sets the accreditation rules for e-invoicing service providers; no list had been published when we wrote this. Already registered? When your CAS needs updating.

Changes to a registered system have their own rules. These are the ones we plan around.

Odoo 20 upgrade checklist
  • Major enhancements

    Adding or removing financial modules, or a version change that affects the financial side, needs an updated registration before you use it. Skip it and the registration has no effect from the date you adopt the change.

  • Minor enhancements

    Screen changes, bug fixes and performance improvements need only a written notice.

  • EOPT invoice changes

    RR 11-2024 treated the EOPT invoicing changes to a registered CAS or CBA as a major enhancement, with an updated registration and a new Acknowledgement Certificate.

  • An old Permit to Use

    A PTU stays valid unless it is revoked or the system has a major enhancement.

  • Sister companies and branches

    Affiliates register separately, even on a shared system. Read BIR set-up for multi-branch businesses.

What is a BIR Computerized Accounting System (CAS)?

A CAS is a computerised system that records a business's transactions, generates its invoices and produces its books of accounts and financial reports. Under RMO 9-2021 a business registers its CAS with the BIR at its Revenue District Office (RDO) or Large Taxpayers office. If the system issues invoices by hand, it counts as Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) instead.

Is there BIR-accredited accounting software?

No. The BIR registers each taxpayer's use of a system, not a software brand. In its public advisory of 14 September 2026 it said it has not accredited any e-invoicing service provider and warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS or CBA and, if the RDO accepts the application, receives an Acknowledgement Certificate. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Do I still need a Permit to Use (PTU)?

No. RMC 5-2021 replaced the Permit to Use with registration and an Acknowledgement Certificate (AC). Existing PTUs stay valid unless revoked or the system has a major enhancement.

How long does CAS registration take?

Under RMC 5-2021, the RDO issues the Acknowledgement Certificate within three working days of a complete application; the timing and the decision are the RDO's. There is no demonstration before you start: the RDO evaluates the system afterwards and can revoke the certificate if it finds gaps, which is why we review the requirements with you before you file.

Can Odoo Online or Odoo.sh be registered as a CAS?

Yes. RMO 9-2021 covers cloud and third-party systems. You and the provider sign a joint sworn statement that the system cannot suppress sales, and the registration is cancelled if the provider contract ends.

Does my Acknowledgement Certificate let me issue e-invoices?

No. Under RMC 98-2026 the AC authorises the system but not e-invoicing. Covered taxpayers also need a Permit to Issue (PTI), then EIS Certification within six months, and the covered groups must issue e-invoices by 31 December 2026.

Do I need to re-register after upgrading Odoo?

A version change that affects the financial side counts as a major enhancement, which needs an updated registration before you use it. Minor changes, such as screen changes or bug fixes, need only a written notice. We plan the upgrade so you can file the updated registration first.

Does TechNext file the CAS application for us?

Your business files at its RDO or Large Taxpayers office, in person or by email. We set up the system, prepare the sample printouts, the audit trail and the system description with you, and stay on call for the RDO's questions. The registration decision is the BIR's.

What is the difference between CAS and CBA?

A Computerized Accounting System (CAS) runs the whole cycle, including system-generated invoices. If the system keeps the books but invoices are issued by hand, it counts as Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) under RMO 9-2021. Odoo can be set up for either. See CAS registration requirements.

When must computerized books be registered in ORUS?

Within 30 days after the close of each taxable year (RMC 3-2023), online through ORUS (RMC 4-2026). For calendar-year taxpayers, computerized books for taxable year 2026 are due by 30 January 2027, and loose-leaf books by 15 January 2027, unless the BIR announces otherwise. Your accountant or RDO confirms the dates for your business. See how to register books in ORUS.
