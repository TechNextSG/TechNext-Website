Does our CAS registration cover e-invoicing?

No. Under RMC 98-2026 a CAS or CBA registration doesn't give e-invoicing authority. You need a Permit to Issue (PTI) first, then EIS Certification within six months of the PTI.

We're an accounting firm. Can we bring a client case?

Yes. Book one meeting per client and join it with them. You leave with that client's Blueprint and a written quote.

How does the set-up meet the 31 December 2026 e-invoicing deadline?

We set up sequenced invoice series per branch, lock issued invoices and prepare the transmission so your system issues e-invoices on or before 31 December 2026 (RR 26-2025, kept by RMC 98-2026). Your accountant or RDO confirms whether you are covered.

Do you connect us to the EIS?

The system we set up has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and TechNext has not built one. If you are covered, we assess the options with you and quote any connection separately.

Do you file with the BIR for us?

No. You or your client apply and file at the RDO. We set up the system and prepare the process flow and sample outputs with you.
