Covered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026: e-commerce sellers classed as small, medium or large, Large Taxpayers Service accounts, large taxpayers, and businesses on a Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) with invoicing software. Micro taxpayers are exempt. You need a Permit to Issue first, then EIS Certification within six months.

What the guidelines ask for.

RMC 98-2026, issued on 22 September 2026, sets the working rules. These shape your Odoo set-up.

Read the BIR rules for your accounting system
  1. 1
    A Permit to Issue (PTI)

    You need a PTI Electronic Invoice before you issue any e-invoice.

  2. 2
    EIS Certification

    Completed within six months of getting the PTI.

  3. 3
    A system that can transmit

    Your system must be able to send the required sales data to the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System; sales reporting itself starts once the BIR issues its rules.

  4. 4
    No edits after issue

    An issued e-invoice cannot be deleted or changed. A credit note covers a decrease; a new e-invoice covers an increase.

  5. 5
    A plan for downtime

    If the system is down, issue a BIR-authorised manual invoice, then replace it with an e-invoice that references it.

  6. 6
    Every branch

    Head office and all branches issue e-invoices; different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs.

BIR e-invoicing, answered.

Check if you're covered
What is the BIR e-invoicing deadline?

Covered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices on or before 31 December 2026. RR 26-2025 set the date and RMC 98-2026 kept it.

Are micro taxpayers covered by BIR e-invoicing?

No. Micro taxpayers are exempt from mandatory BIR e-invoicing, and e-commerce coverage applies to sellers classed as small, medium or large.

What is a Permit to Issue (PTI)?

A Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice is the BIR permit a covered taxpayer needs before issuing any e-invoice. EIS Certification must then be completed within six months of the PTI.

Is Odoo BIR compliant?

No accounting software is certified as BIR compliant: the BIR does not accredit accounting software brands, it has not accredited or endorsed any e-invoicing service provider (BIR advisory, 14 September 2026), and the permit is issued to your business for the system you register. Odoo 20's Philippine localisation covers invoices set up for BIR and CAS requirements, a Book of Accounts, 2306 and 2307 certificates, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and 2550Q. Its release notes do not cover an EIS connection, and TechNext has not built one; we assess what one needs and plan it as separate work. You file your CAS and e-invoicing applications with the BIR yourself. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Can we fix an e-invoice after it is issued?

No. Issued e-invoices cannot be deleted or changed. Use a credit note for a decrease and a new e-invoice for an increase; we set both flows up in Odoo before go-live.

Does BIR e-invoicing cover our branches?

Yes. BIR e-invoicing covers head office and all branches, and branches on different invoicing systems may need separate PTIs. See BIR rules for multi-branch businesses.
