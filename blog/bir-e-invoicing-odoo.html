BIR e-invoicing requirements: who is covered, the deadline and how to prepare in Odoo

The BIR wants covered taxpayers issuing electronic invoices by 31 December 2026. Here is who is covered, what the new rules ask for, and what preparing your Odoo database involves.

Prepared by the TechNext Philippines Odoo and finance team, Taguig City.

Key takeaways

  • Covered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices that meet BIR requirements on or before 31 December 2026, under RR 26-2025 and RMC 98-2026.
  • Covered now: e-commerce sellers (small, medium and large), Large Taxpayers Service accounts, large taxpayers, and businesses on CAS or CBA with invoicing software. Micro taxpayers are exempt.
  • You need a Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice first, then EIS Certification within six months of the PTI.
  • The rule covers your whole company: head office and every branch.

The Philippines is moving invoices online. The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) now requires covered businesses to issue electronic invoices, not just print them from a system. The deadline is close: 31 December 2026. TechNext runs Odoo projects from our hub in Taguig City, so this date sits in every Philippine plan we write. This guide sets out what the rules say, and what it means if you run Odoo.

Where the rules come from

Three issuances matter. RR 11-2025 set the framework for e-invoicing and electronic sales reporting. RR 26-2025, published on the BIR website on 16 October 2025, amended its transition rules and moved the deadline to 31 December 2026 (see KPMG's summary). Then on 22 September 2026, the BIR issued RMC 98-2026 with the working guidelines, and kept the same date (see Grant Thornton's alert and the Daily Tribune report).

Who must comply by 31 December 2026

GroupStatus

Taxpayers using a Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) with invoicing softwareCovered by 31 Dec 2026
Exporters, incentive-registered enterprises, POS users and othersLater, once a separate regulation is issued
Micro taxpayersExempt

The BIR Commissioner can add other taxpayers. If you are unsure which group you are in, check with your accountant or your BIR revenue district office.

What the new guidelines ask for

  • A Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice. You need a PTI before you issue any e-invoice.
  • EIS Certification (Electronic Invoicing and Sales Reporting) within six months of getting the PTI.
  • A system that can transmit. A printed invoice is not an e-invoice unless your system can send the required sales data to the BIR.
  • No edits after issue. You cannot delete or change an issued e-invoice. Use a credit note for a decrease, and a new e-invoice for an increase.
  • A plan for downtime. If your system is down, you issue a BIR-authorised manual invoice. Later you replace it with an e-invoice that references it.
  • Every branch. Head office and all branches must issue e-invoices. Different invoicing systems for different branches may need separate PTIs.

Sales reporting is a separate obligation. EIS stands for Electronic Invoicing and Sales Reporting, and your system must be able to send the required sales data to the BIR, but RMC 98-2026 applies the sales reporting itself only once the BIR issues its rules for it. For background on how the rules took shape, read Grant Thornton's countdown piece, written on the draft guidelines before RMC 98-2026 was issued.

What Odoo's Philippine localisation already covers

Odoo has a Philippine localisation. According to Odoo's documentation, it includes:

  • a chart of accounts and default VAT rates (12%, zero-rated and exempt);
  • expanded, final and VAT withholding taxes;
  • the BIR 2550Q VAT summary;
  • SLSP, SAWT and QAP reports, with .DAT exports for the BIR's ReLiEf and Alpha tools;
  • a BIR 2307 export (as XLS, which you convert to DAT or PDF);
  • a check layout that follows local check standards.

That is a solid base for day-to-day BIR work. The same documentation does not mention e-invoicing or EIS, so there is no switch to turn on.

Common questions about BIR e-invoicing

What is the BIR e-invoicing deadline?

Covered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices that meet BIR requirements on or before 31 December 2026. RR 26-2025 set the date and RMC 98-2026 kept it.

Are micro taxpayers covered?

No. The BIR's guidelines exempt micro taxpayers from the mandatory e-invoicing requirement.

Is my CAS registration enough to issue e-invoices?

No. Under RMC 98-2026 the registration of your CAS authorises the system, not e-invoicing: you also need a Permit to Issue (PTI) Electronic Invoice before you issue any e-invoice, and EIS Certification within six months of the PTI. See BIR CAS set-up.

Does Odoo handle BIR e-invoicing out of the box?

Odoo's Philippine localisation covers taxes, withholding and BIR reports such as 2550Q, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and a 2307 export. Its documentation does not mention EIS. Odoo has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and TechNext has not built one. If you are covered, we assess the options with you and quote any connection separately.
