BIR Form 2307: issuing, collecting and claiming it, and how Odoo produces it

Form 2307 is the certificate that turns tax your customer withheld into a credit you can claim. Chasing them every quarter is one of the most familiar BIR chores in the Philippines. Here is how the certificate works after the EOPT Act, why credits get disallowed, and how Odoo produces 2307s from your vendor bills.

Prepared by the TechNext Philippines Odoo and finance team, Taguig City.

Key takeaways

  • The payor issues Form 2307 to each payee within 20 days after the quarter closes, or with the payment if the payee asks (RR 2-98 as amended by RR 11-2018).
  • Since the EOPT Act, the duty to withhold arises when the income becomes payable, is recorded in the payor's books or is invoiced, whichever comes first (RR 4-2024).
  • The payee claims the credit only if the income was declared and the 2307 proves the withholding. A 2307 for government VAT withheld is a VAT credit only.
  • Odoo 19 exports 2307 data as XLS from bills and payments. Odoo 20 generates the 2306 and 2307 as PDFs in the BIR format.

Every quarter, finance teams across the Philippines do the same thing: send reminders to customers for missing 2307s, then match each certificate to an invoice before the income tax return is due. A missing certificate is a credit you cannot claim. On the other side, your own suppliers expect their 2307s from you on time. This guide covers both sides and how Odoo helps.

What Form 2307 is

BIR Form 2307 is the Certificate of Creditable Tax Withheld at Source. The current form is the January 2018 version. Its sibling, Form 2306, covers final withholding tax.

  • Part I, payee: TIN, name and registered address.
  • Part II, payor: TIN, name and address.
  • Part III, details: each type of income payment with its ATC, the amount for each month of the quarter, the quarterly total and the tax withheld. A separate block covers business tax withheld, such as VAT withheld by government.
  • Signatures: the payor signs, and the payee signs a conforme.

Who issues it, and when

SituationWhen the 2307 is due
Expanded withholding tax on your paymentsWithin 20 days after the close of the quarter (for the quarter ending 31 December 2026, by 20 January 2027)
The payee asks for itAt the same time as the payment
VAT or percentage tax withheld by governmentIssued by the agency as proof of the VAT withheld (RMC 36-2021). Ask for it with each payment; the agency remits the VAT monthly on Form 1600-VT

The EOPT Act did not change the 20-day rule. It did change when the duty to withhold arises: when the income becomes payable. RR 4-2024 treats that as the date the expense is accrued or recorded in your books, or the date the supplier issues its invoice, whichever is earlier. The old "no withholding, no deduction" rule was repealed when the EOPT Act took effect on 22 January 2024, but the duty to withhold and remit remains.

How the payee uses it

  • Income tax credit. The tax withheld is credited against your income tax for the quarter the income was earned or received (RR 2-98 Section 2.58.3). Two conditions: the income is declared in your return, and the 2307 proves the withholding.
  • SAWT. Your 2307s are summarised in the SAWT filed with your quarterly and annual income tax returns (1701Q, 1702Q, 1701 or 1702).
  • Government VAT withheld. Since 2021, the 5% VAT withheld by government is creditable. The agency issues a 2307 with ATC WV010 (goods) or WV020 (services), and you use it against your VAT return. Under RMC 36-2021, using it against income tax leads to disallowance.
  • Your own invoices. Since 16 October 2026, RR 6-2026 requires the creditable withholding tax to appear as a separate item on your invoice, which makes each customer's 2307 easier to match. It does not change when or how the 2307 is issued. See the EOPT invoice guide.

Why 2307 credits get disallowed

  • No certificate on file for the income being claimed.
  • Wrong or missing details: TIN, name or address errors, or no payor signature.
  • Income not declared in the return the credit is claimed in.
  • Timing gaps between the quarter on the 2307 and the quarter the income was reported.
  • Wrong ATC or rate on the payor's side, which then has to be corrected in its 1601-EQ.

Disallowed withholding credits are a frequent issue in BIR audits. Every one of these causes is easier to catch when the certificate, the invoice and the income sit in the same system.

How Odoo produces the 2307

Odoo builds the certificate from the withholding taxes on your vendor bills, so set-up comes first:

  • Every withholding tax carries its ATC on the tax's Philippines tab. Taxes without an ATC are left out of the 2307. See setting up withholding tax and ATC codes.
  • Your company has its TIN with branch code and RDO. Each supplier has its TIN in the Tax ID field, and individuals have first, middle and last names.
VersionHow the 2307 is produced
Odoo 18 and 19Select bills (or a payment) and use Actions > Download BIR 2307 XLS. The file lists the bill lines with withholding, ready to convert to the BIR format. Odoo's documentation notes it does not create the PDF itself.
Odoo 20Under Accounting > Reporting > Tax Report, the 2306 & 2307 report generates the certificates in the BIR PDF format, grouped by ATC per partner, for a month or a quarter. Several certificates download as one ZIP. The release notes add sending the certificate to the vendor when a bill is paid.

For the 2307s you receive, attach each certificate to the customer invoice it covers. Separately, Odoo builds the SAWT from customer invoices that carry sales withholding taxes.

A quarterly 2307 routine

  1. In the first week after quarter-end, generate your outgoing 2307s from Odoo and send them to suppliers before the 20th.
  2. List customer invoices with withholding and the 2307s still missing, and follow up.
  3. Attach each received 2307 to its invoice and check the TIN, ATC and amounts.
  4. Keep VAT 2307s from government separate so they are claimed against VAT, not income tax.
  5. Build the SAWT and QAP once the certificates are in.

Common questions

When must I issue Form 2307 to my suppliers?

Issue Form 2307 to your suppliers within 20 days after the close of each quarter, or at the time of payment if the supplier asks, under RR 2-98 as amended by RR 11-2018.

What is the difference between Form 2306 and 2307?

Form 2307 certifies creditable withholding tax, which the payee claims as a credit. Form 2306 certifies final withholding tax.

Can Odoo print the BIR 2307 as a PDF?

In Odoo 20, yes: the 2306 & 2307 report generates the certificates in the BIR PDF format. Odoo 18 and 19 export the data as XLS for conversion.

Can I claim a government VAT 2307 against income tax?

No. Under RMC 36-2021, VAT withheld by government is credited against your VAT. Using it against income tax leads to disallowance.
