InvoiceNow for GST-registered businesses: the dates, and what to do now

Singapore is making InvoiceNow mandatory for every GST-registered business, in phases that run to April 2031. Here is when your business is due, who is exempt, and how to prepare your accounting system.

Key takeaways

  • All GST-registered businesses must submit invoice data to IRAS through InvoiceNow-Ready Solutions, phased in from 1 November 2025 to 1 April 2031.
  • Existing registrants are phased by total annual supplies in 2025: up to S$200,000 from April 2028, up to S$1 million from 2029, up to S$4 million from 2030, and above that from April 2031.
  • IRAS began notifying businesses registered before 2026 of their date from mid-2026.

InvoiceNow is Singapore's nationwide e-invoicing network, based on the international Peppol standard and introduced by the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) in 2019. Under the GST InvoiceNow Requirement, GST-registered businesses must send their invoice data to the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) through InvoiceNow-Ready Solutions. It is mandatory, and it is being rolled out in phases. TechNext implements Odoo Accounting for Singapore companies, so these dates shape our project plans. This guide sets them out as IRAS publishes them on its GST InvoiceNow Requirement page.

When your business must comply

FromWho it applies to
1 Nov 2025Companies that register for GST voluntarily within six months of incorporation.
1 Apr 2026Businesses that apply for voluntary GST registration on or after 1 April 2026, whatever their incorporation date or business structure.
1 Apr 2028Businesses that apply for compulsory GST registration on or after 1 April 2028, and existing GST-registered businesses with total annual supplies of S$200,000 or less.
1 Apr 2029Existing GST-registered businesses with total annual supplies of S$1,000,000 or less.
1 Apr 2030Existing GST-registered businesses with total annual supplies of S$4,000,000 or less.
1 Apr 2031Existing GST-registered businesses with total annual supplies above S$4,000,000.

Total annual supplies means the value of standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies (Box 4 of the GST return) across all prescribed accounting periods ending in calendar year 2025. IRAS has been notifying GST-registered businesses that registered before 2026 of their implementation date since mid-2026, and offers a GST InvoiceNow Implementation Date Calculator on the same page. If your business has not been notified (for example, because it has no GST returns for periods ending in 2025), IRAS says the date will be communicated later.

Who is exempt

IRAS lists two exclusions from the GST InvoiceNow Requirement:

  • overseas entities, including overseas vendors registered under the Overseas Vendor Registration (OVR) pay-only regime and the OVR full regime;
  • businesses liable to register for GST wholly because of the reverse charge regime.

How InvoiceNow works

Invoices travel over the InvoiceNow network through IMDA-accredited Access Point Providers. Once a business switches on invoice data submission in its InvoiceNow-Ready Solution, IRAS receives a copy of the invoice data through those access points. Businesses on off-the-shelf accounting software use a solution from an accredited InvoiceNow-Ready Solution Provider; businesses with in-house enterprise systems connect through an access point themselves. IRAS publishes the lists of accredited providers, and grants and free-of-charge solution packages are available.

What it means if you run Odoo

The Odoo 20 release notes say Singapore's GST taxes were refined to prepare for future GST InvoiceNow document compliance, and add tax invoice and credit note reports that, in Odoo's words, comply with IRAS GST requirements. That is groundwork. Before your date, you need a confirmed path for sending invoice data from your Odoo database to IRAS through an accredited access point, tested on your real documents.

In a Singapore Odoo project, TechNext helps you work out your implementation date from your 2025 supplies (the IRAS notification is the final word) and sets up your invoices in Odoo. TechNext is not an InvoiceNow Access Point Provider: we work with the IMDA-accredited Access Point Provider you choose, and plan with them how invoice data leaves your Odoo database.

What to do now, whatever your date

  1. Find your date. Check the IRAS notification or the implementation date calculator.
  2. List your invoice flows. Customer invoices, credit notes, and anything issued outside your main system: those are the documents in scope.
  3. Check your system. Ask your software provider or Odoo partner whether your setup is InvoiceNow-ready, and through which access point.
  4. Onboard early. IRAS encourages businesses to onboard early so they can confirm submissions work before the requirement applies.
  5. Clean your master data. Correct customer names and UENs make e-invoicing far smoother.

Rules change. This article reflects IRAS's GST InvoiceNow Requirement page as of September 2026; always check the latest IRAS guidance and e-Tax Guide before acting.

Common questions

Is InvoiceNow mandatory for GST-registered businesses?

Yes. IRAS requires all GST-registered businesses to submit invoice data using InvoiceNow-Ready Solutions, rolled out in phases between 1 November 2025 and 1 April 2031.

When must an existing GST-registered business comply?

It depends on total annual supplies in 2025: S$200,000 or less from 1 April 2028, S$1 million or less from 1 April 2029, S$4 million or less from 1 April 2030, and above S$4 million from 1 April 2031.

Which businesses are exempt from the GST InvoiceNow Requirement?

Overseas entities, including overseas vendors under the OVR pay-only and full regimes, and businesses liable to register for GST wholly because of the reverse charge regime.

Can TechNext help us prepare Odoo for InvoiceNow?

Yes, on the Odoo side. TechNext helps you work out your implementation date and sets up your invoices in Odoo. We are not an Access Point Provider: we work with the IMDA-accredited Access Point Provider you choose, which carries your invoice data over the InvoiceNow network.
