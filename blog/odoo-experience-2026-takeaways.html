Odoo Experience 2026: five takeaways for companies running Odoo

Odoo used its Brussels conference to launch Odoo 20, report its numbers and change its prices. Here is what matters if your company runs Odoo, or is deciding whether to.

By TechNext · 27 September 2026 · 6 min read

Key takeaways

  • Odoo expects about €900 million in billing revenue for 2026, up 45%, and still targets one billion by 2027.
  • AI is now an operational assistant inside Odoo, and the MCP connector links outside AI systems to it.
  • Prices change for the first time since 2022: Standard unchanged, Custom up 20%, One App Free kept.
  • Odoo opened a subsidiary in the Philippines in the first half of 2026.

Odoo Experience is Odoo's yearly conference at Brussels Expo, and the place where each new version is launched. The 2026 edition ran for three days in September and closed a series of earlier events in the United States, Mexico, Kenya and India. Odoo reported nearly 45,000 attendees, more than 450 talks and more than 400 exhibitors. TechNext followed the announcements closely, because they shape what our clients will run for the next year. These are the five that matter most, drawn from Odoo's own recap and the Odoo 20 release notes.

1. Odoo is growing fast, and that is good news for customers

Odoo expects billing revenue of roughly €900 million for 2026, a 45% increase, and it kept its forecast of reaching one billion by 2027. It employs nearly 8,000 people and plans to reach 10,000 by 2027.

Why this matters to a business choosing an ERP: the biggest long-term risk with business software is betting on a product that stalls. A vendor growing at this rate keeps shipping a major version every year, and keeps its partner network and app ecosystem busy. For companies weighing Odoo against larger suites, the conference numbers answer the "will it still be here in ten years?" question.

2. AI moves from chatting to doing

Odoo framed AI in version 20 as more than a conversational agent: an operational assistant that can be trained to carry out tasks and grow new skills. In practice, Odoo 20 lets automated and scheduled actions call AI agents, and agents can create and update records, including from an uploaded PDF. The new MCP connector lets any AI system work with Odoo data.

Two things to plan for. First, every AI feature now uses paid IAP credits, so AI usage becomes a line in the budget. Second, agents are only as good as the process they are given. TechNext's approach is to automate one well-understood step first, such as reading vendor bills, keep a person approving the output, and widen from there. Our Odoo AI integration work follows the same rule.

3. The first price change since 2022

Odoo said it had held prices since 2022 despite 26% cumulative inflation, and that rising AI, hosting and IT costs forced a change. The adjustment is narrower than that suggests:

  • One App Free stays: one app, free, for unlimited users.
  • Standard prices stay the same.
  • Custom rises by 20%. Custom is the plan that includes Studio, multi-company, the external API and hosting on Odoo.sh or on-premise.
  • A new Light User access covers employees who only need HR actions and the apps their job requires.

Odoo's CEO, Fabien Pinckaers, said he believed customers would stay even if prices doubled or tripled, but that he would not do it, calling pricing a matter of accessibility and fairness. For budgeting, the practical point is simple: if you are on Custom, or need it for multi-company or custom modules, include the increase in your renewal. Prices vary by country, so ask TechNext to include the licence estimate for your market in your quotation.

4. A new Odoo subsidiary in the Philippines

Odoo opened two new subsidiaries in the first half of 2026, one in France and one in the Philippines. For Philippine companies this adds a local Odoo presence alongside partners who implement it on the ground. TechNext's own Philippines office in Taguig City, Metro Manila is our main development and consulting hub, where our developers, consultants, finance, sales and marketing teams work, and where we are hiring now. The Odoo 20 Philippine localisation, with BIR 2306 and 2307 certificates and a basic payroll package, points the same way. We cover it in detail in Odoo 20 in Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam.

5. Odoo 20 is the version to plan for

With Odoo 20 launched, the question for existing customers is when to upgrade, not whether. A few changes need attention before any upgrade: the Field Service app is folded into Planning, Payroll drops work entries, and account groups become parent accounts. Custom modules and Studio changes need testing on a copy. Our summary, Odoo 20: what's new, in plain language, lists what to check.

What TechNext is telling clients

For companies not yet on Odoo, nothing in Brussels changes the starting point: map your processes, start with the apps that carry your money and stock (usually Accounting, Sales and Inventory), and choose the plan that fits the first phase. For existing clients, we are scheduling Odoo 20 dry runs on staging copies, with AI features switched on only where a process is ready for it.

Common questions

How many people attended Odoo Experience 2026?

Odoo reported nearly 45,000 attendees at Brussels Expo over three days, with more than 450 talks and more than 400 exhibitors.

Did Odoo raise its prices in 2026?

Partly. Odoo kept One App Free and left Standard prices unchanged, raised the Custom plan by 20%, and added a Light User access type. It was the first change since 2022.

Does Odoo have an office in the Philippines?

Odoo said it opened a Philippine subsidiary in the first half of 2026. TechNext, an Odoo Partner, also has an office in Taguig City, Metro Manila.
