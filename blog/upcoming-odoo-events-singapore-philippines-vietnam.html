Upcoming Odoo events in Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam

Odoo's AI Day, Business Shows and one-day accounting workshops come to Singapore, Metro Manila, Legazpi, Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City in October and November 2026, alongside free online webinars. Here is the list, checked against odoo.com on 27 September 2026.

At a glance

  • Singapore: Odoo AI Day on 22 October and an Accounting Essentials masterclass on 23 October, both at Guoco Midtown.
  • Philippines: an AI Business Show in Makati on 6 October, a Business Show in Legazpi and an accounting workshop in Quezon City on 20 October, and a workshop in Sta. Rosa on 27 October.
  • Vietnam: Vietnamese-language accounting workshops in Hanoi (27 October) and Ho Chi Minh City (29 October), and Business Shows for service companies on 17 and 19 November.
  • Every event listed here is free on its odoo.com page; registration is required.

Odoo 20 launched at Odoo Experience in Brussels in September, and the next few months are when Odoo takes it on the road. For companies in Southeast Asia, the local events are the easiest way to see the new version live, meet Odoo's regional team and ask questions about your own setup. TechNext has offices in Singapore, Taguig City and Ho Chi Minh City, so we have collected the events in all three countries in one place. Every entry links to its page on odoo.com/event, where you register.

Elsewhere in Southeast Asia

Odoo also runs Business Shows in the region's other markets: the Penang Supply Chain Management Business Show on 13 October (AC Hotel Penang by Marriott), the Odoo Business Show 2026 in Tangerang on 20 and 21 October (Swiss-Belhotel Serpong, in Bahasa Indonesia) and the Odoo Trading Business Show in Petaling Jaya on 24 November (Hotel Armada Petaling Jaya). All three are free, with registration on odoo.com.

What about Odoo Experience 2027?

Odoo has not announced dates for its 2027 conferences yet. The 2026 editions ran in San Francisco and Mexico City on 2 September, Nairobi on 3 September, Gandhinagar on 11 September and Brussels from 24 to 26 September. In Southeast Asia, Odoo's regional formats are the Business Shows, AI Days and Academy workshops above. We summarised the Brussels announcements in Odoo Experience 2026: five takeaways.

How to get the most out of an Odoo event

  1. Pick the right format. Business Shows and AI Days are for deciding whether Odoo fits; Academy workshops are hands-on days for people who will use it.
  2. Bring one real process. A recent order, a month-end close or a stock count gives you concrete questions to ask.
  3. Check the language and requirements. Several workshops are in Vietnamese, and the hands-on days need a laptop.
  4. Register early. Two of the Philippine events were already sold out when we checked.

Can't make it? TechNext can walk you through the same Odoo 20 features on your own data in a discovery session, from our offices in Singapore, Taguig City or Ho Chi Minh City.

Dates, times, venues and availability are as listed on odoo.com/event on 27 September 2026. Events can change or fill up, so check the event page before you go.

Common questions

Are Odoo events free?

Every event in this list is marked free on its odoo.com page, and each one requires registration. Some sell out, so register early.

Where can I find all upcoming Odoo events?

Odoo lists its events, webinars and workshops at odoo.com/event, where you can filter by country and register.

Is there an Odoo Experience in Asia?

Odoo has not announced an Asian edition. In Southeast Asia, its regional events are Business Shows, AI Days and one-day Academy workshops.

Can TechNext show us Odoo 20 if we can't attend an event?

Yes. TechNext runs discovery sessions from Singapore, Taguig City and Ho Chi Minh City, where we map your processes and show the Odoo 20 features that fit them.
