    common questions

    Working at TechNext, answered.

    Where are TechNext's open roles based?

    All six current roles are in Taguig City, Metro Manila, at our Philippines office (Level 9, IP Center), our main development and consulting hub. TechNext is headquartered in Singapore and has an AI engineering hub in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.

    Are the roles hybrid?

    Four of the six are listed as hybrid: the Functional ERP/Odoo Consultant, B2B Sales Consultant, Senior Accountant and HR Generalist. Each JobStreet listing states the work arrangement for that role.

    Do I need Odoo experience?

    For the ERP roles, yes: the Senior Solutions Architect and Functional Consultant listings ask for hands-on Odoo implementation experience. The other listings set out their own requirements, such as ERP or SaaS sales experience for the sales role.

    How do I apply for a job at TechNext?

    Open the role on JobStreet from this page and apply there with your CV. If there is no listing for the role you want, email career@technext.asia.
