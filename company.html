Nexi · in one paragraph

TechNext Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202699888G) is a technology consultancy headquartered at 261 Waterloo Street, Singapore, with its main development and consulting hub in Taguig City, Metro Manila and an AI engineering hub in Ho Chi Minh City. An Odoo Ready Partner, TechNext implements Odoo ERP (Odoo 20) end to end and builds enterprise AI (agents, knowledge systems and assistants) for companies that want manual work out of the way. why odoo, and why a partner

One system is easier to run than five.

Most growing companies run sales in one tool, stock in another and accounts in a third, joined by spreadsheets. Odoo puts them in one database. An Odoo Partner implements it with Odoo's methodology and direct access to Odoo's partner resources — so you get the standard product configured well, not a bespoke system nobody else can maintain.Sales, stock and accounts in one Odoo database, implemented by a partner with Odoo's methodology: the standard product, configured well.

common questions

About TechNext, answered.

Where is TechNext based?

Our headquarters is at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261. Our main development and consulting hub is at Level 9, IP Center, Taguig City, Metro Manila, and our AI engineering hub is at 62 Nguyễn Thị Nhung, Phường Hiệp Bình, Ho Chi Minh City. We work on-site in Singapore and remotely with clients in 10+ countries.

Is TechNext an Odoo partner?

Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory. We implement Odoo end to end: discovery, configuration, data migration, training, integration and support.

What does TechNext do besides Odoo?

Enterprise AI: agents and workflow automation, knowledge assistants that answer from your own documents, and chatbots for the web and WhatsApp, often connected to Odoo. We also build websites and run social media as a monthly service, and deliver IoT solutions, mobile and web apps, and office networks.

What is TechNext's registered name and UEN?

TechNext Pte. Ltd., UEN 202699888G, registered in Singapore.

Is TechNext hiring?

Yes. We have open roles in Taguig City, Metro Manila, for Odoo and ERP consultants, finance, HR, sales and marketing. See them on our careers page, or email career@technext.asia.

How do we start working with TechNext?

Tell us how your process runs today by email to sales@technext.asia, on WhatsApp, or through the quotation form. Most Odoo projects start with a discovery session that maps your processes to Odoo apps.
