why odoo, and why a partner

One system is easier to run than five.

Most growing companies run sales in one tool, stock in another and accounts in a third, joined by spreadsheets. Odoo puts them in one database. An Odoo Partner implements it with Odoo's methodology and direct access to Odoo's partner resources — so you get the standard product configured well, not a bespoke system nobody else can maintain.

Where is TechNext based?

Is TechNext an Odoo partner?

Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory. We implement Odoo end to end: discovery, configuration, data migration, training, integration and support.

Enterprise AI: agents and workflow automation, knowledge assistants that answer from your own documents, and chatbots for the web and WhatsApp, often connected to Odoo. We also build websites and run social media as a monthly service.
