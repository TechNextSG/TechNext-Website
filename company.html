Where is TechNext based?

TechNext is headquartered at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261. Our main hub is at Level 9, IP Center, Taguig City, Metro Manila, and our engineering hub is in Ho Chi Minh City.

Is TechNext an Odoo partner?

Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner in Singapore, listed in Odoo's partner directory. We implement Odoo end to end. We are not affiliated with, accredited by or acting for the BIR or any government agency, and we do not file or register on your behalf.

Do we have to replace our current system to use Odoo?

No. TechNext sets up Odoo as a plug-in in your system. Your current POS, accounting software or legacy ERP stays, data comes in by API, daily batch sync or flat-file upload, and Odoo maps transactions to the correct ATCs and withholding rates without double entry. We start with Accounting and BIR set-up. You can add more Odoo apps later.

Does TechNext implement Odoo in Singapore?

Yes. TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner headquartered in Singapore. We set Odoo up for Singapore GST and prepare your invoices for InvoiceNow, working with the IMDA-accredited Access Point Provider you choose. IRAS phases InvoiceNow in for existing GST-registered businesses from 1 April 2028 to 1 April 2031. See GST InvoiceNow dates.
