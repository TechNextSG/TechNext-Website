No. TechNext sets up Odoo as a plug-in in your system, beside your current accounting software, POS or spreadsheets, and starts with Accounting and BIR set-up. You can add more Odoo apps later.

Does TechNext implement Odoo in Singapore?

Yes. TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner headquartered in Singapore.
