Pricing and scope

Fast, Transparent Implementation.

You don't need a million-peso ERP overhaul to become BIR compliant. Pricing scales based on your branch network and integration complexity.

  • Single entityStandard 5-Day CAS set-up* for one TIN on clean, reconciled ledgers. Our starting scope.
  • Multi-branchAdds a sequenced invoice series and reports per branch.
  • IntegratedAdds an API, batch or flat-file link to the POS or software you keep.

*Standard 5-day technical deployments available for clean, reconciled data ledgers. Odoo's own subscription is separate and is paid to Odoo.

Do my clients have to replace their current system?

No. Their POS, accounting software or legacy system keeps running. Transactions come in by API, daily batch sync or flat-file upload, and the BIR reports are built from them.

Is a 5-day set-up realistic?

For clean, reconciled ledgers, yes: the 5 days cover the technical deployment. Messy or unreconciled data is cleaned first, and that time is written into the quote before anything starts.

How are ATCs and withholding rates assigned?

Each vendor, product and expense type is mapped once to its ATC and rate during set-up. From then on every bill carries the right code into the 2307, SAWT and QAP.

Can the books be edited after posting?

No. Posted entries are locked and can only be reversed. Every change before posting is logged with the user and a timestamp.

Who does the work?

TechNext's own consultants and developers in Taguig City and Singapore. The expert on your readiness call works on the set-up. Meet the experts.

Is TechNext linked to the BIR?

No. TechNext is not affiliated with the BIR. We set up the system and prepare the documents with you; your RDO handles the registration on its own timeline.
