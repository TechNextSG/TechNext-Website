    common questions

    Odoo and TechNext, answered.

    What does TechNext do?

    TechNext implements Odoo ERP end to end (discovery, configuration, data migration, training, integration and support) and builds enterprise AI, websites and social media for growing companies.

    Is TechNext an official Odoo partner?

    Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory.

    Where is TechNext based?

    Our headquarters is at 261 Waterloo Street #03-36, Singapore 180261. We also have an office in Taguig City, Metro Manila and a development hub in Ho Chi Minh City, and serve clients in 10+ countries.

    How much does an Odoo implementation cost?

    There are two parts: Odoo licences, priced per user per month by Odoo, and the implementation work, which TechNext quotes in writing. The quote depends on the apps, number of users, data to migrate, integrations, custom modules and training.
