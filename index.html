    BIR EIS (E-Invoicing) deadline

    31 Dec2026

    days left

    RR 11-2025 as amended by RR 26-2025; RMC 98-2026

    31 December comes sooner than you think.

    E-invoicing through the BIR's EIS is due by 31 December 2026 for CAS and CBA users, large taxpayers and e-commerce sellers. Micro taxpayers are exempt; your accountant confirms whether your business is covered.

    1. December is short.Christmas, New Year and year-end leave take working days out of the month, for your team, your accountant and your RDO.
    2. The last-minute rush is slower.Applications and questions pile up at the BIR as the deadline nears, so processing can take longer. Submit early.
    3. Missing it has a cost.Under RR 11-2025 (Sec. 5), not following the e-invoicing rules is subject to the penalties in Sections 264 and 264-A of the Tax Code. For failing to issue proper invoices, Sec. 264(a) sets a fine of ₱1,000 to ₱50,000 and 2 to 4 years' imprisonment for each act, on conviction.

    BIR and TechNext, answered.

    Do we need a CAS or a CBA?

    RMO 9-2021 draws the line at invoices. If your system issues them, it is a Computerized Accounting System (CAS); if invoices are issued by hand and the system keeps your books, it is Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA). Large taxpayers must use a CAS. Odoo Online and Odoo.sh are registered too, with a joint sworn statement with the provider. Confirm with your accountant or RDO.

    Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

    No software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business can register Odoo as its CAS or CBA at its Revenue District Office. TechNext is an Odoo Ready Partner, not affiliated with or acting for the BIR. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

    Can TechNext set Odoo up for our BIR CAS registration?

    Yes, on the Odoo side. We set Odoo up for your Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) application and prepare the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail printout with you. You register your system at your Revenue District Office; the decision, and any Acknowledgement Certificate, is the BIR's. How BIR CAS set-up works.

    What does Odoo produce for the BIR?

    Odoo's Philippine localisation produces the 2307 per payee, the SLSP, SAWT and QAP as DAT files for the BIR's tools, and the 2550Q VAT summary, from the invoices and bills you post. Your Books of Accounts export from the same entries for your yearly ORUS registration.
