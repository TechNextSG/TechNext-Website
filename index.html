Questions we hear first

Do we have to replace our current system?

No. TechNext adds Odoo as a plug-in in your system: you keep your current accounting software, POS or in-house system, and Odoo produces the books and BIR forms from the data we bring in. Later, Odoo can grow app by app.

How much does it cost?

We keep it affordable for SMEs: you can start with Odoo Accounting only. After the call you get a fixed, written quote for your scope before any work starts. Odoo's subscription is separate and is paid to Odoo.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

No accounting software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand, and it has warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS or CBA at its Revenue District Office. TechNext is not affiliated with or acting for the BIR. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

Can TechNext set Odoo up for our BIR CAS registration?

Yes, on the Odoo side. We set Odoo up for your Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Computerized Books of Accounts (CBA) application and prepare the sample invoices, books, reports and audit trail printout with you. You file at your RDO; the decision is the BIR's. How BIR CAS set-up works.

How long does it take?

The Odoo set-up runs as the 5-Day Express: five working days, after a call and a process map. Day 1 sets up Odoo, Day 2 the BIR settings, Day 3 loads your data, Day 4 tests every output and Day 5 is hand-over and training. The sprint starts once your data is ready; bigger scopes run as extra sprints, written into your fixed quote. Your RDO sets its own timeline for the CAS registration. If you need your books ready for ORUS registration by 30 January 2027, tell us early.

What is the NextSprint method?

It is how TechNext runs every set-up: Agile daily sprints with a check-in at the end of each day, Lean scope (standard Odoo and only the BIR outputs you need first) and Six Sigma checks (a checklist for every output, tested against your sample books before hand-over). See the method.
