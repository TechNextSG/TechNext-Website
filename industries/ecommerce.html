    Bank feed and reconciliation

      Payouts are matched here as they arrive.

      Orders in: the website runs on Odoo eCommerce; two marketplaces send orders and delivery slips through a marketplace connector. Every order becomes a sales order in Odoo and reserves stock.

      Fulfilment: each channel ships from its warehouse, or from the other one when it is short; website orders get a carrier label, marketplace orders a label from the connector; tracking goes to the customer and the channel. Returns are refunded, then restocked or scrapped.

      Stock sync: every stock change goes back to all channels, so a listing shows sold out before it can be oversold. With spreadsheet updates, marketplaces keep selling stock that is gone.

      Payouts: providers and marketplaces pay out net of fees and refunds; each bank line is matched to its orders in Accounting, with the fees booked to their own account.

      Sample shop, fees and timings: a day of orders runs in a couple of minutes. Marketplace connectors differ by marketplace, so TechNext confirms what each one syncs and sets up the warehouse rules, reconciliation models and fee accounts with you.

      common questions

      Odoo for online sellers, answered.

      Can Odoo connect to Lazada and TikTok?

      Yes. Odoo's release notes list marketplace integrations with Lazada and TikTok that fetch orders and delivery slips and synchronise inventory.

      Can Odoo stop overselling across channels?

      Yes. Orders from the website and connected marketplaces draw on the same stock, and inventory is synchronised back to the marketplaces.

      Does Odoo print shipping labels?

      Yes. Through Odoo's carrier integrations, labels and tracking numbers are generated from the delivery order.

      Can we move our existing online store to Odoo?

      Yes. Products, customers and order history can be migrated, and TechNext plans redirects so the store keeps its search rankings.

      With where orders come from today: each channel, how stock is shared between them and how payouts reach the bank.
