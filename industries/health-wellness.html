        what usually slows you down

        Three problems we see first.

        Bookings in one app, money in another

        Appointments live in a booking tool; payments and packages are tracked somewhere else; they never agree.

        Packages tracked by hand

        Sessions remaining, expiries and membership renewals kept in spreadsheets or on cards.

        Retail on a separate till

        Products sold at the counter never reach the same stock or accounts as the services.

        common questions

        Odoo for studios, spas and gyms, answered.

        Can Odoo manage gym or studio memberships?

        Yes. Memberships and class packs run as subscriptions that bill and renew automatically, and Odoo 20's Frontdesk app can limit check-ins to members or to specific tiers.

        Can clients book classes and treatments online?

        Yes. Odoo Appointments publishes booking pages by service, staff member or room, with capacity limits and reminders.

        Can the front desk sell products and packages?

        Yes. Point of Sale sells retail products, gift cards and packages, with loyalty points and eWallets that work across visits.

        Can Odoo show recurring revenue?

        Yes. Subscriptions reports show recurring revenue, renewals and churn, and every payment posts to Accounting.
