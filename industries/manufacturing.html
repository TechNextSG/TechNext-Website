common questions

Odoo for manufacturers, answered.

Does Odoo handle multi-level bills of materials?

Yes. Odoo supports multi-level bills of materials, kits, by-products and operations on work centers, and Odoo 20 adds BoM comparison in PLM.

Can operators use Odoo on the shop floor?

Yes. The Shop Floor app runs on a tablet at each work center, where operators see instructions, record quantities with barcodes and log their time.

How does Odoo calculate product cost?

Component costs, work center time and extra costs registered on the bill of materials roll up to each manufacturing order, and the stock valuation posts to Accounting.

Can Odoo trace lots and serial numbers?

Yes. Lots and serial numbers are tracked from receipt through production to delivery, and the traceability report shows where each one went.

Where does TechNext start with a manufacturer?

With one product family: its bill of materials, operations and components. Once it runs end to end, the rest of the catalogue follows.
