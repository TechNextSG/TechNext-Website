      Lot traceability and recallClick a lot on the shelves

      Shelves: saline, gauze and hand sanitiser held in lots, each with an expiry date, an alert date 30 days before it and a removal date 7 days before it.

      Picking: FEFO reserves the lot with the earliest removal date first. FIFO reserves the lot received first, so a short-dated lot received later can reach its removal date on the shelf.

      Alerts and quarantine: the alert date raises an activity for the stock controller; at the removal date the lot moves to a quarantine location that is never reserved for picking.

      Recall: the traceability report follows one lot back to its supplier receipt and forward to every transfer, delivery and customer, so the remaining stock can be blocked, customers contacted and the lot returned to the supplier.

      Sample data and product settings. Odoo records the lots and dates, applies the removal strategy and raises the alert; quarantine moves, customer contact and returns are steps your team confirms. Clinical records stay in your medical record system.

      Odoo for clinics, answered.

      Is Odoo a medical records (EMR) system?

      No. Odoo runs the business side of a clinic: appointments, rosters, stock, purchasing, billing and accounting. Clinical records stay in your EMR, and TechNext can connect the two where your EMR offers an API.

      Can Odoo track medicine expiry dates?

      Yes. Inventory tracks lot numbers and expiry dates, can alert before expiry, and picks first-expiry-first-out at each branch.

      Can patients book appointments online?

      Yes. Odoo Appointments publishes booking pages by doctor, service or branch, sends reminders and syncs with staff calendars.

      How is access to data controlled in Odoo?

      Access rights limit each role to the records it needs, and TechNext sets them up with you. Clinical notes belong in your EMR, not in Odoo.

      Can Odoo handle several clinic branches?

      Yes. Each branch can have its own stock locations, rosters and P&L in one database, or its own company for separate legal entities.

      Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

      No software is BIR-accredited in that sense: the BIR registers your books and issues your Permit to Issue for the system you use, and its advisory of 14 September 2026 says it has not accredited any e-invoicing service provider. What matters is that your books, invoices and withholding meet BIR requirements. TechNext sets Odoo up for them and advises on what applies to your company. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

      Yes.
