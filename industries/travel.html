In one paragraph

Odoo for travel businesses keeps enquiries, itineraries, supplier costs, deposits and margin in one place. Each booking is a quotation built from supplier services, confirmed with a deposit, paid to suppliers from purchase orders and closed with its real margin, in any currency.

          Itinerary: flights, hotel nights, transfers and tours are supplier services with a cost in the supplier's currency, placed on each day of a five-day Tokyo and Kyoto trip.

          Price and margin: costs in SGD, USD, JPY and EUR convert at sample rates; a markup per category sets the selling price, and the waterfall runs from supplier cost through markup and price to the margin left.

          Sample services, costs and exchange rates; taxes left out. In Odoo, a markup like this is a pricelist rule based on cost, services can raise purchase orders when the quotation is confirmed, and the deposit is a prepayment on the online quotation.

          common questions

          Odoo for travel businesses, answered.

          Can Odoo handle multi-currency travel bookings?

          Yes. Quotations, invoices and supplier bills can each be in their own currency, and Odoo posts exchange differences automatically when payments are reconciled.

          Can clients sign and pay a deposit online?

          Yes. Odoo quotations can be signed online and paid through a payment link, and a down payment can be invoiced first with the balance later.

          Can Odoo show the margin on each trip?

          Yes. With supplier costs recorded against the booking, each trip shows revenue, cost and margin, and reports compare margin by product line or salesperson.

          Does Odoo replace a GDS or booking engine?

          No. Odoo manages enquiries, quotations, suppliers, payments and accounting. Airline and hotel inventory still comes from your booking tools, and TechNext can connect them where an API exists.

          Where does TechNext start with a travel business?

          With one recent booking, end to end: enquiry, quotation, deposit, supplier bookings, payments and margin.
