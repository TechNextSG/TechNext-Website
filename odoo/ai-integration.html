    questions

    What teams ask before they start.

    Your question isn't here? Ask at the pass

    Will it post to the ledger on its own?

    No. The AI prepares — it reads and matches bills, drafts entries and suggests actions — but a person approves anything that posts, sends money or contacts a customer.

    What can it see in Odoo?

    Only the records the task needs. Access is scoped up front, and every action it takes is logged against the record in Odoo for audit.

    Do you implement Odoo as well?

    Yes. As an Odoo Partner we implement the ERP and build the AI that runs inside it, so both are handled by the same team.

    How do we know it's accurate?

    It's measured on your own documents and records before it's allowed to run unattended, and lookups show the source so people can verify.

    Can our data stay in our environment?

    Yes. Where data can't leave, we deploy on-premise or private-cloud models, and choose the model and hosting for your data requirements.

    What does it cost?

    Pricing is on quotation, scoped to the task and your Odoo setup. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
