How the Odoo apps share data on one database.

Every line is a native link between two Odoo apps: no connector, no re-keying. Select an app to see what it talks to, or filter by category.

Odoo app mapNative links between Odoo apps

Select an app · drag to move

Sales, Inventory and Accounting: confirmed orders create deliveries, delivered quantities are invoiced, stock moves post valuation entries, and CRM opportunities become quotations.

Supply chain: Manufacturing consumes and produces stock, buys missing components through Purchase, and works with Quality, PLM and Maintenance. Point of Sale updates stock and posts each session to accounting.

People and services: Employees feeds Payroll, Time Off, Expenses, Recruitment and Appraisals; Project works with Timesheets and Planning, which runs field service from Odoo 20; Website runs eCommerce, Blog, Forum and eLearning; Email Marketing, Social Marketing and Marketing Automation bring leads into CRM.

Standard links between Odoo apps (Odoo 17 to 20). What moves on each link depends on the apps and settings you switch on.

Which Odoo apps should we start with?

Most companies start with Accounting, then Sales and Inventory, the apps where money and stock move. In the Philippines, Accounting comes first for BIR CAS set-up.

Can we add Odoo apps later?

Yes. Every Odoo app shares one database, so adding an app later needs no integration project.
