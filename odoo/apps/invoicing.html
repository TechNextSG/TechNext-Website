Odoo Invoicing in one paragraph

Odoo Invoicing: send invoices and collect payments online. TechNext, an Odoo Ready Partner headquartered in Singapore with teams in the Philippines and Vietnam, sets it up on a staging copy of your database, migrates your data, trains the people who use it and supports it after go-live.

In Odoo 20, invoice reminders go out from the invoice list or run automatically, duplicate bills and invoices get two levels of warning, and one download zips every file made for the selected invoices. Payment statuses are renamed: "In Process" becomes "Paid" and "Paid" becomes "Reconciled". Odoo 20 by country.

For Philippine companies: BIR

Invoices set up for the BIR invoice rules under the EOPT Act, with a series per branch, and, if you are a covered taxpayer (deadline 31 December 2026), a plan for e-invoicing. Odoo has no built-in connection to the BIR's EIS, and TechNext has not built one. If you are covered, we assess the options with you and quote any connection separately.
