    what it does

    Rental, section by section.

    The product as Odoo presents it. TechNext configures each part to your process.

    01 · Rental

    Save time, rent online

    Online booking and reservations have never been easier thanks to integrations with Odoo's eCommerce application. Customers can shop available products, reserve their selected dates, and checkout right on the website.

    04 · Rental

    Automate your to-do-list

    With one click, know exactly who is picking up and who is dropping off. Stay on top of your day by tracking the status of rentals and invoices from one easy dashboard.

    Time is money … literally

    Whether you rent by the hour, week, month or year, create customized pricing for every product based on different time frames.

    Signature requests on request

    Upload rental agreements, insurance forms, contracts, and more, request a customer signature digitally before any products ever leave your warehouse.
