Odoo Timesheets in one paragraph

Odoo Timesheets: time tracking billed to projects. TechNext, an Odoo Ready Partner headquartered in Singapore with teams in the Philippines and Vietnam, sets it up on a staging copy of your database, migrates your data, trains the people who use it and supports it after go-live.

In Odoo 20, a timer in the top menu logs time from any screen, and the ActivityWatch integration turns device activity into timesheets while keeping the data private. The Timesheets dashboard compares billing targets with billable time. What changed in Odoo 20.
