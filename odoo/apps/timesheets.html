    what it does

    Timesheets, section by section.

    The product as Odoo presents it. TechNext configures each part to your process.

    01 · Timesheets

    Start the work. Stop the timer .

    Smart time tracking. The timer starts automatically the moment you check in. Just stop it when you're done, link your time to a project, and your timesheet is done!

    02 · Timesheets

    Timesheeting, no longer time-consuming

    All in one place. Check your timesheets, and those of your team members, in a glimpse.

    03 · Timesheets

    Automated timesheets, zero effort

    Stop wasting hours tracking minutes. The timesheet assistant securely monitors your desktop and browser activity to build accurate timesheets automatically.

    04 · Timesheets

    Time is money

    Keep it profitable. Get an overview of your billable time by team member, project, task, and billing type.

    05 · Timesheets

    Save even more time with AI

    Get rich insights. Ask Odoo's integrated artificial intelligence for employee timesheet updates, team performance reports, average logged hours, and more.
