      Leads arrive from a website form, an email alias, WhatsApp, an event attendee list and LinkedIn, and become leads in Odoo CRM.

      Rules route them: scoring sets a probability and a priority; a territory rule sends Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia leads to the Singapore team and Philippine leads to the Philippines team; a product-line rule sends warehouse projects to the Projects team; round-robin gives each lead to the next salesperson with capacity.

      The pipeline runs New, Qualified, Proposition, Won, with lost deals archived with a reason. Each stage schedules the next activity; a lead with no owner or no progress is flagged as stale and the team lead is alerted. A won deal opens a quotation in Sales with the customer and products carried over.

      Reports: conversion from stage to stage, and a weighted forecast of expected revenue times probability.

      questions we hear

      Before you decide.

      Isn't Odoo CRM fine out of the box?

      It's a solid pipeline as-is. The value is in matching it to how you actually sell — your stages, your routing, your fields — and connecting it to Sales so nothing gets re-keyed.

      Where do leads come from?

      Website forms, email aliases, WhatsApp and social can all create leads automatically. We set up capture for the channels you use and route each lead by your rules.

      How are leads assigned?

      By whatever rule fits — round-robin, territory, product or deal value. Scheduled activities then make sure every opportunity has an owner and a next step.

      What happens when a deal is won?

      The won opportunity opens a quotation with the customer, products and pricelist already filled in. From there it flows into Sales, Inventory and Accounting on the same record.

      Can we use AI in the pipeline?

      Yes, optionally. Odoo can draft follow-ups and enrich contacts for your team to review before anything is sent. See AI Integration.

      How is this priced?

      It depends on your pipeline, lead sources and integrations, so it's scoped and quoted. Walk us through your last ten deals and we'll come back with a plan — pricing is on quotation.
