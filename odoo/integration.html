Sync log

    Connected to Odoo: bank feeds (statement lines, on a schedule), a payment gateway (payment events by webhook), a marketplace connector (orders by webhook, stock levels on a schedule), a courier (labels out, tracking by webhook), e-invoicing through an access provider (e-invoices out, delivery status polled), a BI or data warehouse (scheduled extracts) and a legacy WMS (polled for picks and confirmations).

    Reliability: each message carries an idempotency key, so a re-delivered event is recognised and ignored instead of creating a second order or payment. A failed call is retried with exponential backoff, 1, 2, 4 then 8 seconds in this demo; after the last try it moves to a dead-letter queue and a person is alerted. When the connector recovers, the backlog drains and dead letters can be replayed safely.

    Do we have to replace our current accounting software or system?

    No. TechNext adds Odoo as a plug-in in your system, so your team keeps working as now while Odoo takes over accounting and the BIR outputs. You can move more across later, or never.

    What data moves into Odoo?

    Usually invoices, bills, payments, customers, vendors, your chart of accounts and bank statement lines. We agree each flow in writing before anything is switched on.

    What stays in our own system?

    Anything you want to keep there: day-to-day entry, specialist tools, POS or in-house software. TechNext only brings in the records Odoo needs for accounting and BIR.

    How long does it take to add Odoo as a plug-in?

    It depends on your current system, the data and which outputs you need first. TechNext sets the schedule in a short adviser meeting and writes it into the quote.

    How much does adding Odoo as a plug-in cost?

    Pricing scales based on your branch network and integration complexity. You can start with Accounting only and get a written quote before any work starts. Odoo's own subscription is paid to Odoo. Talk to a compliance engineer to start.
