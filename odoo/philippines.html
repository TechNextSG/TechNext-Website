a year of BIR filings

BIR compliance in Odoo for Philippine companies, month by month.

How often each BIR form comes round, and where Odoo produces it: 2307 and 2306 certificates from vendor bills, the 1600-VT report, the monthly 1601-C payroll items, the 2551Q for non-VAT companies each quarter, and the yearly 2316 and 1604-C with its Alphalist DAT file.     what odoo covers

    Odoo 20 for Philippine companies.

    The Philippine localisation in Odoo 20 is the most complete it has been. This is what it covers, from Odoo's release notes.

    Accounting and BIR

    • BIR 2306 and 2307 certificates as PDFs, generated on vendor bills and sent when a bill is paid
    • BIR- and CAS-compliant invoices, a BIR-format partner ledger and a Book of Accounts
    • The 1600-VT report for final withholding VAT and the 2551Q for non-VAT companies
    • Senior citizen and PWD discounts on invoices and credit notes

    Payroll

    • A basic Philippine payroll package: basic pay, benefits, taxes and overtime
    • Form 1601-C items for monthly BIR payroll reporting
    • The yearly BIR Form 2316, and 1604-C worksheets with Alphalist DAT exports
    • Automatic SSS and Pag-IBIG loan deductions

    Retail and restaurants

    • GrabFood orders and menu sync in the Point of Sale
    • Lazada and TikTok marketplace connectors for online sellers
    • Loyalty, gift cards and self-ordering at the counter or table

    Groups across countries

    • A company per country in one database, each with its own localisation
    • Philippine and Singapore entities side by side, with intercompany transactions
    • Multi-currency accounting with automatic exchange differences

    Sources: the Odoo 20 release notes. The release notes do not mention the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System (EIS); if your company is covered by EIS, we plan that connection as its own piece of work. Read more in Odoo 20 in Singapore, the Philippines and Vietnam.

    common questions

    Odoo in the Philippines, answered.

    Is TechNext an Odoo partner in the Philippines?

    Yes. TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner, listed in Odoo's partner directory, with an office at Level 9, IP Center, Taguig City, Metro Manila.

    Can Odoo generate BIR Form 2307?

    Yes. Odoo 20 generates official BIR 2306 and 2307 withholding tax certificates as PDFs from vendor bills, and can send them to the vendor when a bill is paid.

    Does Odoo support Philippine payroll?

    Odoo 20 adds a basic Philippine payroll package with basic pay, benefits, taxes and overtime, plus Forms 1601-C, 2316 and 1604-C with Alphalist DAT exports, and automatic SSS and Pag-IBIG loan deductions.

    Does Odoo 20 support the BIR's Electronic Invoicing System (EIS)?

    The Odoo 20 release notes do not mention EIS. If your company is covered by it, TechNext plans the connection as a separate piece of work during discovery.

    Can one Odoo database run our Philippine and Singapore companies?

    Yes. Odoo runs a company per country in one database, each with its own localisation, currencies and reports, with intercompany transactions between them.
