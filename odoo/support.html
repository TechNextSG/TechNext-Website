      Support, answered.

      Do you support Odoo you didn't build?

      Yes. A short review of the current setup comes first, so we know what we are taking on before we commit to a plan.

      What are your response times?

      Response targets are agreed in your plan and tracked against every ticket. We keep them realistic to your setup rather than quoting a headline number that doesn't fit.

      How do we raise requests?

      Through a single channel, where each request becomes a tracked ticket from open to done, so nothing gets lost in an inbox.

      Will an upgrade break our customisations?

      We test on a staging database, migrate your customisations and upgrade on a schedule you choose, never a forced cut-over.

      Where is the support team based?

      Support is led from our Singapore HQ, with consultants and developers at our main development and consulting hub in Taguig City, and an engineering hub in Ho Chi Minh City. We serve clients globally.

      What is TechNext Care?

      TechNext Care is our monthly plan after go-live, in three levels: Essentials, Growth and Full team. You do not need to hire your own Odoo team.
