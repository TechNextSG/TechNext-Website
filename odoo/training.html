In one paragraph

Odoo training from TechNext is role-based: each team learns its own screens on a copy of your own data. Finance learns bank reconciliation and month-end, sales learns quotations and confirmations, and the warehouse learns receipts, picking and counts, with written guides left behind for hand-over.

      How the plan is built: each role (accountant, sales, warehouse, buyer, manager, administrator) brings its Odoo modules: invoices, vendor bills, bank reconciliation and month-end for finance; pipeline, quotations, order to invoice and the portal for sales; receipts, picking, adjustments, cycle counts and reordering for the warehouse.

      Each module has three steps: a session, a sandbox exercise on a staging copy of your own data, and a sign-off check the user must pass alone.

      The order of sessions: key users first, then end users, then train-the-trainer so key users can onboard new staff, then UAT and a go-live readiness check. A refresher follows the first month-end.

      Sample plan. Durations, exercises and the readiness score are illustrative; your plan is sized to your teams and agreed before the first session.

      Ground school · questionscommon questions

      Training, answered.

      Is training on a demo or on our own system?

      On a staging copy of your database, not a demo. People learn on your real products, customers and layouts, so nothing looks unfamiliar on go-live day.

      On-site or remote?

      Both. We run sessions on-site in Singapore and remotely for teams in other locations.

      Who should attend?

      Administrators first, then each team for its own screens. We train key users deeply so they can help train the rest.

      What is UAT?

      User acceptance testing: your team runs real tasks end-to-end before go-live, so questions and gaps surface while there is still time to fix them.

      Can we train new hires later?

      Yes. The quick-reference guides let you onboard without us, and we can run refresher sessions whenever the team grows or Odoo changes.

      How much does training cost?

      On quotation, sized to the teams and sessions involved — request a quotation.
