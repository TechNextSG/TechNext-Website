What changed for companies in Vietnam.

Four rules touch an Odoo setup in Vietnam. Here is what applies now, with the source for each.

  1. From 1 July 2026E-invoices

    New e-invoice rules from 1 July 2026

    • Decree 254/2026/ND-CP took effect on 1 July 2026, with Circular 91/2026/TT-BTC
    • It replaces Decree 123/2020/ND-CP, as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP
    • Household businesses with yearly revenue over VND 1 billion must use coded e-invoices or e-invoices from a cash register linked to the tax authority
    • If a buyer gives no name or ID, the invoice must say "Sold to consumer"

    Sources: EY Vietnam, KPMG Vietnam.

  2. To 31 December 2026VAT at 8%

    VAT at 8% until 31 December 2026

    • Many goods and services taxed at 10% are taxed at 8% from 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2026
    • Set by Resolution 204/2025/QH15, with Decree 174/2025/ND-CP on how to apply it
    • Not covered: telecoms, banking, finance, securities, insurance, real estate, metals, most mining, and goods under special consumption tax (petrol aside)

    Sources: Vietnam Law Magazine, DFDL.

  3. From 1 January 2026Chart of accounts

    Circular 99 replaces Circular 200

    • Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC, issued 27 October 2025, replaces Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC
    • It applies to financial years starting on or after 1 January 2026
    • Companies can tailor their chart of accounts, and some accounts are added or removed
    • Financial statements are still prepared in VND

    Sources: Duane Morris, Vietnam Briefing.

  4. VietQRBank payments

    QR bank payments

    • VietQR codes let customers send money to an account at any NAPAS member bank
    • The VietQR standard follows EMVCo's QR payment standard and the State Bank's basic QR code standard
    • Customers scan the code in their own banking app and confirm the transfer

    Source: NAPAS.

Summaries, not legal advice. Which rules and rates apply to your company is confirmed with your accountant during discovery.

  • Vietnamese and Singapore entities side by side, with intercompany transactions
  • Multi-currency accounting with automatic exchange differences

Odoo in Vietnam, answered.

Is TechNext an Odoo partner?

TechNext Pte. Ltd. is an Odoo Ready Partner in Singapore, listed in Odoo's partner directory. Our Vietnam office is an engineering hub at 62 Nguyễn Thị Nhung, Phường Hiệp Bình, Ho Chi Minh City. Odoo configuration and custom modules are built by our Taguig City hub, with our Singapore HQ.

Does Odoo follow Circular 99 in Vietnam?

Yes. In Odoo 20, the Vietnamese chart of accounts and balance sheet follow Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC, and the S03a-DN general journal and S03b-DN general ledger now show counterpart accounts. Circular 99 replaces Circular 200 for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2026.

Can Odoo issue e-invoices in Vietnam?

Yes. Odoo sends invoices to SInvoice, Viettel's e-invoice platform, including replacement and adjustment invoices. In Odoo 20, Point of Sale can also issue SInvoice e-invoices for POS orders.

Which decree governs e-invoices in Vietnam now?

Decree 254/2026/ND-CP, in force since 1 July 2026 with Circular 91/2026/TT-BTC. It replaces Decree 123/2020/ND-CP as amended by Decree 70/2025/ND-CP. Odoo's documentation does not mention the new decree yet, so we check your invoice setup against it during discovery.

Is VAT in Vietnam still 8%?

For many goods and services, yes, until 31 December 2026. Resolution 204/2025/QH15 cut the 10% rate to 8% from 1 July 2025, with some sectors left out, such as telecoms, banking, insurance and real estate. We set the rate per product with your accountant.

Can Odoo export the 01/GTGT VAT declaration?

Yes. Odoo 20 adds the Tax Declaration Form 01/GTGT and its Appendix 142, and exports both as XML for import into HTKK.

Can one Odoo database run our Vietnamese and Singapore companies?

Yes. Odoo runs a company per country in one database, each with its own localisation, currencies and reports, with intercompany transactions between them.
