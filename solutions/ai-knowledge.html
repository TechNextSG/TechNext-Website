In one paragraph

An enterprise RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) knowledge assistant reads your policies, manuals, contracts, tickets and ERP records and answers questions from them in plain language, citing the source of each answer. TechNext builds these assistants to stay inside your approved documents and respect each user's permissions, so nothing is invented.

same question, different access

Enterprise RAG that respects who is asking.

A TechNext knowledge assistant embeds the question, searches your SOPs, policies, product specs and Odoo records, ranks the closest passages, then drops every passage the person asking may not see, before a word of the answer is written. Pick a question and a role to watch the answer change, and move the relevance bar to see when it refuses to guess.

      questions

      What teams ask before they start.

      How does it avoid making things up?

      It answers only from passages retrieved from your own sources, and cites them. When the documents don't cover a question, it says so rather than guessing.

      Could it show a document someone shouldn't see?

      No. Retrieval is permission-aware: who can see a document decides who can get an answer from it, following your existing access rules.

      What sources can it read?

      Policies, manuals, contracts, wikis, support tickets, PDFs and Odoo records — connected from the systems where you already keep them.

      Where can our people use it?

      Inside Odoo Discuss or Knowledge, in Microsoft Teams or Slack, in a web widget on your intranet, or on WhatsApp for a scoped customer version.

      Does our data leave our environment?

      It stays in your tenancy. Where data can't leave, we deploy private-cloud or on-premise models and choose hosting for your requirements.

      What does it cost?

      Pricing is on quotation, scoped to your sources and channels. Request a quotation and we'll return a written scope.
