What the marketing service covers

TechNext runs websites and social media for companies as a monthly service: strategy, design, production and a one-page report. Take one part or all three (website, social media and brand assets) and the branding stays consistent across everything, while your team stays on its own work.

Website and social media marketing, measured from first visit to won deal.

Channels to stages: Search, Social, Email, Referral and Events bring visits; visits become leads, leads become marketing-qualified (MQL), then sales-qualified (SQL), then won deals.

Calculator: the monthly paid budget sets paid visits on top of organic and referral traffic; the four conversion rates set how many move to each stage; cost per lead and cost per won deal follow.

Attribution: first touch credits the channel that brought the first visit, last touch credits the final touch before each stage, and linear shares the credit across every touch. The same deals, credited three ways.

Sample data. Rates, costs and journeys are illustrative, not benchmarks: your own figures come from your analytics and CRM. Leads onward are drawn at a larger scale than visits so the later stages stay visible.

Good to know.

Can I take just one of the three services?

Yes. A website, social media management or brand assets each work on their own. Most companies start with one and add the others as they need them.

Do I need to be a client of your other services?

No. Marketing is a standalone service you can take entirely on its own. Nothing else is required to work with us on it.

How is it priced?

Websites and brand assets are usually a one-off; social media management is a monthly retainer. Every engagement is scoped and quoted first, so you see the figure before you commit. Get a quotation.

Do we approve work before it's published?

Always. Pages, posts and assets are shared for your sign-off before anything goes live.

Who owns the files and the accounts?

You do. Websites, social accounts, analytics and editable master files stay in your name, handed over completely.

Where is the team based?

Singapore is home base, with a global production team. We have worked with clients across 10+ countries and transformed 11+ enterprise clients.

Tell us what the website or the channels need to do.

We'll propose a scope in plain terms, with a monthly figure or a one-off quotation. Starting Odoo with us? Your website and brand come with it.
