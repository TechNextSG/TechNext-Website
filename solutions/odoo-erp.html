    Before you commit.

    Can Odoo handle BIR reporting?

    Yes, TechNext sets up Odoo's Philippine localisation for your company. Odoo produces the 2307, SLSP, SAWT, QAP and 2550Q from the invoices and bills you post, and can serve as your computerised books. Odoo has no built-in EIS connection, and TechNext has not built one; if you are covered, we assess options and quote separately.

    Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

    No accounting software is BIR-accredited in that sense. The BIR registers your books and issues your Permit to Issue for the system you use. We set Odoo up for BIR requirements; your business registers it as its books. See BIR rules for your accounting system.

    How long does an Odoo implementation take?

    It depends on the apps, data, entities and integrations involved. We set the timeline in discovery and write it into the quote.

    Which apps should we start with?

    Usually the ones causing the most manual work today, often Accounting, Sales and Inventory. We confirm the order in discovery.

    Can we keep some of our existing systems?

    Yes. That is the idea of Odoo as a plug-in in your system: if a system works, we connect Odoo to it instead of replacing it.

    What happens to our historical data?

    Customers, vendors and products come across in full. For transactions we usually bring opening balances and open items, and keep the old system for history.

    Do you build custom features?

    Only when standard Odoo cannot meet a real need. Custom work is a separate module, so it survives upgrades.

    Where will Odoo run?

    On Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or your own infrastructure, depending on custom modules and data-residency rules.

    TechNext gives you a written quote before any work starts, scoped to the apps, data and integrations you need. Starting with Accounting only keeps it affordable for an SME. Get my Odoo plan and tell us what is manual today.
