          Plan: a month of LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram posts, each tagged with a content pillar: expertise, behind the scenes, projects, offers and events.

          Approve: every post goes draft, in review, approved, scheduled, published. You approve or ask for changes; a post that reaches its day without approval is held back, never published unapproved.

          Publish and measure: published posts build impressions, engagements and link clicks; tracked links carry UTM tags, so website enquiries and messages passed to your sales contact arrive in Odoo CRM as leads, and sales qualifies them into opportunities.

          Sample data for an illustrative month. Scheduling and lead tracking are shown in Odoo Social Marketing and Odoo CRM; your own tools work too. Your accounts stay yours.

          common questions

          Good to know.

          Which channels do you manage?

          LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram. We recommend the right mix for your business, and you decide which to run.

          Do we approve posts before they go live?

          Yes. Every post is shared for your sign-off before it is scheduled or published.

          Do you write the captions and make the graphics?

          Yes. Design, copy and short video are all part of the service, produced in your branding.

          What happens with comments and messages?

          We check and respond on working days, and pass any leads to your sales contact so nothing is missed.

          Do you need access to our accounts?

          We need the access required to post and reply, but the accounts stay in your name — ownership is always yours.
