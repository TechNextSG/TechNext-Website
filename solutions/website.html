In one paragraph

TechNext designs and builds company websites, campaign landing pages and online stores: short copy, real information and fast, mobile-first pages. Every site includes SEO basics, analytics and accessibility, is built to be edited without us, and is wired so each inquiry reaches the right person on your team.

build it live

Web design and development, from sitemap to CRM lead.

A TechNext website build starts with the sitemap, assembles each page from sections, checks speed and SEO before launch, then wires the contact form into your CRM (Odoo CRM in this example). Add or remove pages and change the build choices: the wireframe, the Core Web Vitals and the SEO checks react. Then follow an enquiry from its UTM source to a salesperson's follow-up call.

                common questions

                Good to know.

                Can we edit the site ourselves afterwards?

                Yes. We build on platforms your team can update, and we hand over notes and a short walkthrough so you are not dependent on us for everyday changes.

                Where is the site hosted, and who owns it?

                Wherever you prefer — your own account or one we set up for you. The domain, the site and the accounts stay in your name.

                Do you do SEO?

                SEO basics are built in: clean titles and descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and fast, accessible pages. Ongoing SEO or content campaigns are a separate scope we can discuss.

                Where do form inquiries go?

                Straight to the inbox or CRM you name, so the right person sees every enquiry as it arrives.

                Can you redesign our existing site?

                Yes. Send us the current site or a blank page — either is a fine starting point, and we will tell you what to keep.

                How is it priced?

                Per scope, as a one-off build. We quote before any work begins, so you see the figure first. Get a quotation.
